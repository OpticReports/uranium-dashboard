# Distress-signals study: data manifest (frozen download)

All files retrieved **2026-10-12**. Downloaded files are untrusted data: parse them, never execute them. Python that reads them runs as `python3 -I`.
Access was anonymous over HTTPS through the session proxy with a generic browser User-Agent. No personal identifier was sent in any request.
FRED CSVs use `fredgraph.csv?id=ID&cosd=1700-01-01`. **Do not use `cosd=1900-01-01`**: it silently drops pre-1900 observations. That truncated 14 series on the first pass, including USREC, all the NBER failure series and the Cowles index.
The scripts that produced `derived/` lived in the session scratchpad and are not committed. Each derivation is described in the file table and in section 6, so it can be rebuilt from the raw files here.
Size: about 35 MB, 434 files. Most of that is `uscourts/` (21 MB; F-5A county tables 9.9 MB). **Not committed to git:** the raw `uscourts/` spreadsheets (21 MB; parsed into `derived/`) and `dol_ar539/ar539.csv` (13 MB, DOL ETA 539 weekly state claims, added 2026-10-12 from https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/csv/ar539.csv; sha256 db7dc790662f7feed975651f2f44d44c86b7541d30b776c9621db8b5e8d81ffb; layout `dol_ar539/eta539_layout.pdf`). Re-fetch from the URLs listed and check the sha256.
The same table is available in machine-readable form in `MANIFEST.csv`.

## 1. Verdict

* **Bankruptcy filings are well covered back to 1980.**
  * Quarterly business filings: 1980Q1 onward (ABI's compilation of US Courts/AO data).
  * Quarterly non-business filings by chapter: 1994Q1 onward.
  * AO Table F-2, business vs non-business × chapter × 93–94 districts: quarterly from 2001Q1 and monthly from 2013-01.
  * Epiq national commercial filings by chapter: monthly from 2006-01.
  * Every AO table parsed cleanly and ties out. District rows sum to the national total, and business totals match ABI exactly in 100 of 100 overlapping quarters.
* **Business failures before 1980 come from Dun & Bradstreet.**
  * Monthly 1894–1968 via the NBER Macrohistory series mirrored on FRED.
  * Annual 1955–1997 from ERP Table B-96.
  * The series has hard methodology breaks in 1933, 1934–39, 1940 and 1984, so it cannot be spliced with the court filings.
* **Household distress:** the NY Fed CCP covers 2003Q1–2026Q2, including new bankruptcy notations and flows into 30+/90+ by product, but state detail exists for only 11 states.
* **Bank loan quality:** FRED delinquency and charge-off rates start in 1985, 1987 or 1991 depending on the loan category.
* **Not openly available:**
  * Moody's and S&P speculative-grade default rates.
  * S&P Global Market Intelligence large-bankruptcy counts and sector splits (published as article text only).
  * Epiq monthly *files* after 2022-03; after that date there is press-release text only.
  * Subchapter V counts, except through FJC microdata or Epiq press releases.
  * Sector (NAICS) detail for business bankruptcies from any official source.
  * The LoPucki BRD: free, but its terms restrict use to academic research. Casey has to decide whether to accept them; the agent did not.
* **Statistical power is the binding constraint, not data.** Court-filing history covers 6 NBER recessions (1980, 1981–82, 1990–91, 2001, 2007–09, 2020), and the 1980 recession has no pre-period in the data. In 2020 filings *fell* because of policy support. The D&B monthly series (1894–1968) adds 17 earlier recessions, but under a different bankruptcy law and different definitions.

## 3. Structural breaks any test MUST handle

1. **Bankruptcy Reform Act of 1978**, effective 1979-10-01. Chapters 7/11/13 replaced the old Act; the ABI series start in 1980 for this reason. Pre-1980 court counts are not comparable, and D&B "failures" are a different concept altogether.
2. **BAFJA, Jul-1984.** Added the §707(b) "substantial abuse" test and court-jurisdiction changes.
3. **Chapter 12 created in late 1986.** There were 6,125 Ch 12 cases in 1987, which is consistent with the 1987Q2 spike in business filings (26,384). Ch 12 farm cases count as business filings.
4. **Bankruptcy Reform Act of 1994** (Oct-22-1994). Raised the Ch 13 debt limits and added a small-business Ch 11 track.
5. **BAPCPA**, enacted 2005-04-20 and effective 2005-10-17. Filers rushed in before the deadline:
   * Non-business filings were 532,526 in 2005Q3 and 654,633 in 2005Q4. They then collapsed to 112,685 in 2006Q1.
   * Business filings were 12,798 in 2005Q4 and 4,086 in 2006Q1.
   * The Ch 7 share fell permanently, from about 80% to about 58%.
   * Treat this as a level and composition break. Never compute YoY changes across 2005–06.
6. **SBRA / Subchapter V**, effective 2020-02-19. The debt limit moved several times:
   * $2.73M at launch → $7.5M under the CARES Act (2020-03-27).
   * Lapsed 2022-03-27 → restored 2022-06-21.
   * Lapsed again in June 2024 to the lower base limit.
   * H.R. 7730 (a permanent $7.5M limit) passed the Senate on 2026-09-28 and is awaiting signature, according to the Epiq/ABI release of 2026-10-08.
   * Each of these changes moves Subchapter V and small-business Ch 11 counts mechanically.
7. **COVID policy, 2020–21.** Bankruptcies and delinquencies fell while unemployment spiked: a false-negative episode for distress indicators. Causes include:
   * forbearance under CARES §4013 and the interagency TDR relief, which changes how bank delinquencies are reported;
   * foreclosure and eviction moratoria, stimulus payments and PPP;
   * the student-loan payment pause (Mar-2020 to Sep-2023) and the credit-reporting on-ramp to 2024-09-30. Student-loan delinquencies were suppressed on credit reports from 2020Q2 to 2024Q4 and jumped back in 2025.
8. **D&B methodology:**
   * 1933 redefinition excluding real-estate/insurance brokers and holding/finance companies; Bradstreet–Dun merger in Mar-1933.
   * Jun-1934 to 1939 segment includes Section 77-B reorganizations.
   * **Mar-1940 coverage expansion** (applied to data from 1939) to voluntary discontinuances with loss and to small failures: a level break between segments b and c. HSUS p. 909 dates the revision to 1939.
   * 1940 SIC reclassification.
   * **1984 expanded coverage and new methodology** (ERP: "not generally comparable").
   * Series discontinued after 1997.
   * Monthly liabilities from 1894 include **bank** failures (m09033a).
9. **Call Report reform, Mar-2001.** Before it, small banks could use internal loan classes, so the Fed imputes those splits. **FAS 166/167 in 2010Q1** brought securitized card loans back onto balance sheets, which breaks the card rates.
10. **District changes.** Arkansas E and W are reported combined as `AR` from Jul-2018 (2018Q3), which takes the count from 94 to 93 districts. `GUAM` was relabelled `GU` in 2021. The F-2 column layout also changed in 2018Q3: a "Chapter 12" column became "other chapters (9, 12, 15, 304)". Before 2018Q3, Ch 9/15/304 appear only in the total.
11. **Units.**
    * AO counts *cases*: a joint petition is one case.
    * NY Fed counts *consumers*: both joint filers are counted.
    * Epiq "commercial" is not the same as AO "business". Annual 2006–2021, Epiq commercial runs 1.23–1.72× AO business. Monthly 2013-01 to 2022-03, Epiq commercial Ch 13 runs 3.0–7.4× AO business Ch 13 (median 4.7×), while Epiq commercial Ch 11 runs 0.68–1.03× AO business Ch 11 (median 0.88×).
    * Related-entity filing clusters inflate Ch 11 counts (e.g. Feb-2024; Sep-2025 included 109 related filings).
12. **Seasonality.** All filing counts are NSA, with a strong March–April (tax-refund) peak. Epiq workbooks report filing days per month.
13. **Revisions.**
    * AO: "the bankruptcy database is continually updated". The 12-month tables exceed the sum of first-print quarters by up to 5% for business Ch 11 (12 months to 2008-09-30: 7,962 vs 7,567).
    * NY Fed: the CCP history is restated each release (2026Q2 revised 2026Q1 card balances; 2026Q2 mortgage balances were hit by a servicer-transfer reporting gap).
    * Epiq: annual totals move ≤1.2% between vintages.
    * EBP: model-based, so history can change when the model is re-estimated.
14. **Shutdown.** October-2025 unemployment rates are missing nationally and for all 51 states.

## 4. Not downloaded or not available (and why)

* **Moody's / S&P speculative-grade default rates.** Paywalled or behind registration; no open monthly series exists. Open proxies in this folder: GZ/EBP, BAA10YM, BAA–AAA, FDIC failures, AO business Ch 11. The ICE HY OAS is already in the dashboard. If Casey has S&P or Moody's access, the annual default-study tables are a **missing input to request, not to approximate**.
* **S&P Global MI monthly large-bankruptcy counts and sector splits.** Article text only, with no file. For example, 372 filings in H1-2026, the highest since 2010; May and June 2026 each had 72. These figures come from secondary press and were not verified against S&P.
* **Florida-UCLA-LoPucki BRD** (large public-company cases, 1979–2022). The download requires accepting terms limited to academic research at subscribing institutions, plus non-disclosure. These terms were *not* accepted. Updates ended in Dec-2022.
* **FJC IDB microdata.** Too large for the repo (2.36 GB zipped). It is openly downloadable and the only open source of case-level county, Subchapter V and asset/liability detail. Debtor geography fields for FY2008 to Jun-2021 were corrected in Nov-2021.
* **Epiq monthly 2022-04 onward as data.** Only press-release text is available (archived in `epiq_press/`). Automatic regex extraction was tried and rejected as unreliable. For monthly 2013–2026 by district and chapter, use AO F-2 one-month instead.
* **HSUS 1857–1970 D&B failure rate.** Image-only PDF and no OCR tool in this environment. **BLS BED establishment deaths:** download.bls.gov returns 403 unless the User-Agent carries contact details, so it was skipped under the no-identifier rule. **Census BDS** (annual, ~2-year lag) was skipped because it cannot lead anything.
* **Sector (NAICS) detail for business bankruptcies.** Not published by AO or FJC. The only open sector splits are the historical D&B manufacturing vs trading series (1894–1958).

## 5. Data-integrity flags found in this audit (act on these)

* **Dashboard mislabel.** `backend/app/config.py:138` maps `delinq_cre` to `DRSREACBS`, and `backend/app/metrics/severity.py:188` labels it "CRE delinquency". FRED titles DRSREACBS "Delinquency Rate on **Loans Secured by Real Estate**", which includes residential mortgages. The CRE series is `DRCRELEXFACBS` (delinquency) / `CORCREXFACBS` (charge-off), from 1991Q1.
* **ABI typos, confirmed against AO tables:**
  * 2001Q3 non-business Ch 13: ABI 105,000 vs AO 105,100.
  * 2003Q1 non-business Ch 7: ABI 285,154 vs AO 285,883.
  * 2010Q1 non-business total: ABI 373,551 vs AO 373,541.
  * 2011Q1 non-business total: ABI 353,082 vs AO 353,802.
* **ABI inconsistencies with no second source:**
  * 1995 quarterly business filings sum to 50,878 against a printed total of 51,878.
  * 1999Q3 non-business chapters do not add up (Ch 11 = 2,283 is an outlier; the chapter sum exceeds the total by 8,782).
* **ABI vs AO, 2019Q1–2020Q4.** The split of non-business Ch 7 vs Ch 13 differs by up to 337 cases. Totals agree.
* **Epiq.**
  * The "Chapter 11 Summary" sheet means *all* Ch 11 in the 2012/2017 vintages but *commercial only* in the 2022 vintage. Use the Business Summary blocks.
  * Epiq commercial totals step up roughly 25–30% in early 2016, with no matching move in AO business filings.
  * The 2026-10-08 release prints YTD individual filings as +7%. The levels it reports (444,252 vs 399,379) imply +11.2%.
* **Releases vs final counts.** First-print AO quarterly business Ch 11 counts were later revised up by 3–5% in 2008–09. A real-time test must use the first prints (`f2_3month`), not the later 12-month tables.

## 6. Derived files: how they were built and checked

* **ABI PDFs.** Converted with `pdftotext -layout` and parsed with regexes. Checks: quarters vs printed totals, chapters vs totals, annual vs quarterly. Every mismatch is listed in section 5.
* **AO F-2.** Spreadsheets were read with xlrd/openpyxl and the two PDFs with pdftotext. The numeric columns were fixed from the TOTAL row, which skips the empty spacer columns in the 2013/2015 files. The old/new layout was detected from the order of the Ch 13 column. Checks:
  * district sums equal the national row, and business + non-business equals the total, in all 102 + 102 + 162 tables, with zero failures;
  * the three monthly tables sum exactly to each quarter's 3-month table;
  * business totals equal ABI's in all 100 overlapping quarters.
* **Shared columns.** `total_other_or_ch12` holds Ch 12 in the old layout and Ch 9+12+15+304 in the new one. `district_harmonized` maps ARE/ARW to AR and GUAM to GU; `state` holds postal codes, with MP for NMI.
* **Epiq.** Business Summary blocks by month. Dec-2012 vintage for 2006–2012, Mar-2022 vintage for 2013-01 to 2022-03; Dec-2017 vintage columns are kept for revision checks.
* **NY Fed.** Positional extraction of pages 12, 13, 14, 17 and 37–40. Pages 13 and 14 are 4-quarter moving sums. Student-loan flows are blank before 2004.
