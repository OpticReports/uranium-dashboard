# Labelling standard (amendments 1 and 7, docs/DESIGN_SECTOR_TIER.md)

Given to every labeller and adjudicator verbatim. Labellers see only a
company's name, its industries and its FMP description.

A company is **GENOMICS** when a material part of its business is in
genetic medicines, genomics tools or genomic diagnostics:

- **gene editing**: CRISPR, base editing, prime editing, other editors;
- **gene therapy**: AAV, lentiviral or other gene transfer;
- **RNA, mRNA and oligonucleotide medicines**: mRNA vaccines or
  therapeutics, siRNA/RNAi, antisense, other oligonucleotides;
- **cell therapy**: CAR-T, TCR-T, TIL, NK, and engineered or
  stem-cell-derived cell therapies;
- **cancer vaccines**, including neoantigen and personalised vaccines;
- **sequencing and genomics tools**: sequencers, genomic, single-cell or
  spatial tools, synthetic DNA, genomic data platforms;
- **genomic and molecular diagnostics**: genetic testing, liquid biopsy,
  molecular/PCR diagnostics, prenatal and oncology genomic profiling.

"Material" means a marketed product, a lead or late-stage programme, or
the company's core platform. A large company is GENOMICS when a marketed
product or a phase 3 programme in one of these modalities is a material
part of its business. Example: Vertex, which co-owns Casgevy, an approved
CRISPR therapy.

**NOT GENOMICS** covers companies with none of these activities. Example:
Kiniksa (antibody and protein drugs for inflammatory disease). Small
molecules, antibodies, ADCs, other proteins, radiopharma, devices,
services, insurers, providers, CROs/CDMOs and pharmacies are NOT GENOMICS
unless they also meet the bar above.

When the standard does not clearly decide, label it the way an investor
focused on the genetic-medicine and genomics sector would, and mark the
confidence low.
