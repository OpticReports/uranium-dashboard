## Verdict

**The idle-cash mechanism IS measurable, and it is real. What this data cannot adjudicate is whether to hold the sleeve at all.** Those are two different claims and the first draft of this document merged them into one wrong one — *"this data cannot adjudicate the mechanism in either direction"* — by measuring a +0.0120 mechanism against the resolution of a comparison that is not a test of it. Against its own counterfactual (the identical book, same weight, same rebalance rule, with the sleeve's idle cash left DEAD), D1-10 is **+0.0147 Sharpe, 95% CI [+0.0116, +0.0183], p<0.0001** — and it clears the 22-arm Bonferroni bar (99.773% CI [+0.0100, +0.0204]). On the sleeve alone the same fix is worth +0.0850 Sharpe [+0.0645, +0.1103] and +184 bps of CAGR. See [the mechanism section](#the-mechanism-against-its-own-counterfactual-m1).

**NOTHING SURVIVED the registered bar.** All 7 registered round-5 arms (D1..D4) failed it against the best incumbent (`INC-B5`). The registration said this was the expected outcome and said so before the arms were built: *"This round is expected to produce another null, and that expectation is on the record."* It is the finding, and it is reported as one. No consolation winner is nominated, no bar is softened, nothing was re-parameterized after a result was seen. 6 of the 7 arms DO beat the incumbent on criterion 1, by margins of +0.0037 to +0.0380 Sharpe against intervals of +/-0.064 to +/-0.198. Criterion 2 fails on every arm, and it is what carries the null: those intervals are wide because the bar's comparison — this book against a book with NO sleeve — carries the sleeve's entire idiosyncratic variance, not because the cash mechanism is unmeasurable.

**Multiple comparisons, across BOTH rounds: 22 arms — and the correction that matters is not in any of them.** Round 5's 7 arms were designed by reading round 4's 15 results, so the honest denominator is the campaign, not the round. Three statements, in the order of how much work they do:

1. **Bonferroni is INERT here and should not be presented as if it dispatched a near-miss.** The smallest raw two-sided p anywhere in the campaign is 0.358 (`C5`) — two orders of magnitude from the naive alpha=0.05, let alone from the adjusted alpha=0.00227 (99.773% CI). Nothing is close, in either round: arms clearing the all-rounds bar **NONE**, arms clearing the round-only bar **NONE**.
2. **Bonferroni is also the wrong instrument for these arms**, which are near-identical portfolios whose tests are massively correlated, so it over-corrects. The step-down max-T (Westfall-Young) resampling correction on the SHARED draw sequence across all 22 arms gives an adjusted p of **0.899** for the best positive arm (`D2`, +0.0380) and **0.760** for the largest statistic anywhere in the campaign (`C5`, -0.1079, on the losing side of zero). Same conclusion, honestly obtained.
3. **The multiplicity that actually matters is in no denominator at all:** the selection of R2-A itself across rounds 1-3, B.5's hindsight-fitted weights, and the choice of window. Twenty-two arms is the SMALL multiplicity here, and no resampling correction computed inside this window can reach the large one.

**The caveat that keeps this honest, stated with the number and not after it:** a paired comparison between two books that differ only by "idle cash earns BIL instead of 0%" is close to an accounting identity, because BIL's daily return was >= 0 on essentially every day of this window. p<0.0001 here is not a discovery — it is the point. A mechanism that is arithmetic does not need a noisy portfolio test to license it, and describing it as unresolvable is what would wrongly stop the work. It is also in-sample like everything else here, and it still assumes idle cash earns a full day of BIL from the prior close with no execution lag and no cost on the implied daily movement, which is why the coverage/drag rows are printed beside it.

## Honesty box

- **The prior was registered and it held.** The registration stated before any code existed that D1 and D2 are ADJACENT to arms that already failed, that the expected uplift was ~+0.0120 Sharpe, and that the honest expectation was another null. That is what happened. This is not a vindicated forecast — it is a round that was not worth much power to begin with, run anyway because the mechanism was mechanical rather than searched. And because it is mechanical, it IS resolved against its own comparator (+0.0147, p<0.0001): the null is about the sleeve, not about the cash.
- **Resolution is per arm, and the registered bar cannot see the cash mechanism — but the mechanism's own comparison can.** Half-widths against `INC-B5` run from +/-0.064 to +/-0.507; the smallest detectable edge across all arms is +0.06 at 50% power and +0.09 at 80%. A +0.0120 mechanism is invisible to THAT test, and no arm here is evidence against the routing. Measured like-for-like it is +0.0147 [+0.0116, +0.0183]. An earlier draft of this box said the round "cannot see its own hypothesis" full stop; that was wrong, and it is the correction this revision exists for.
- **D2's verdict was decided by an UNREGISTERED convention (M3).** The 30% warm-up default is not in D2's contract and it held on 81 days. Judged on the registered low leg the arm goes from 1 of 4 criteria to 3 of 4 and still fails criterion 2 (p=0.505). Disclosed rather than buried, because it is the same class of fault round 4 was corrected for.
- **The sleeve and the 30/70 baseline are modelled BOTH ways (M4).** The frozen (dead-cash) curve is the pinned input to rounds 1-4 and is unchanged; the swept convention — what `ibkr-executor/app/blend.py` actually implements — is reported beside it and is worth +0.085 Sharpe on the sleeve and +0.040 on the 30/70 book. Published R2-A statistics on the dead-cash convention UNDERSTATE the live design.
- **Multiple comparisons span both rounds, and the correction is inert.** 22 arms have now been run against the same incumbent on the same 1433 days, so a discovery would need a 99.773% interval to exclude zero — but the smallest raw p in the campaign is 0.358 (`C5`), so nothing is within two orders of magnitude of even the naive bar. The max-T (Westfall-Young) adjustment on the shared draws, which respects how correlated these near-identical portfolios are, gives 0.899 for the best positive arm. **The multiplicity that matters is in no denominator here at all:** R2-A's own selection across rounds 1-3, B.5's hindsight-fitted weights, and the choice of window.
- **In-sample status is unchanged and it is total.** `barbell-lab/config/portfolio.yaml` fixed B.5's weights on 2026-08-11 with no derivation record; the data ends 2026-08-19. The comparator was chosen with hindsight over exactly this window. The R2-A sleeve carries survivorship, universe/alias hindsight and exit-engine overfit risk, and its 30% weight came from an H13 sweep against a SPY core. No CAGR here is a forecast.
- **The idle-capital input is a rebuild, not a measurement of the live book.** `data/r2a_exposure.json` comes from re-running the round-2 pipeline; it matches the frozen curve on all 2,672 days to <1e-6, but it inherits every assumption of that pipeline. D1 and D2 also assume idle cash earns a full day of BIL from the prior close with no execution lag and no transaction cost on the implied daily cash movement — favourable assumptions that make D1 a CEILING on the cash fix, not a floor.
- **The synthetic window is a FLOOR.** Proxy splice, unadjusted, no manager alpha; **1078 of 2510** return-days use a proxy for at least one constituent (73% of B.5's weight sits in funds that are proxied somewhere in this window). Earlier drafts quoted "1079 of 2511" — a hard-coded string inherited from an earlier round; this figure is counted (m2). Criterion 4 is again not binding, because B.5 alone is the weakest comparator there.
- **What was NOT modeled:** taxes; bid-ask and market impact beyond a flat 10 bps per side; borrow, margin or liquidity constraints; the 20% short-vol bot the config actually pairs B.5 with; PHYS premium/discount; fund-closure or capacity risk; the tail-sleeve question (#1), still open and still held at TAIL.
- **Nothing is promoted.** No config change, no live weight. There are no survivors to nominate; had there been, they would have become HYPOTHESES.md entries at most. Refinements go to a round-6 registration, not into this one.
