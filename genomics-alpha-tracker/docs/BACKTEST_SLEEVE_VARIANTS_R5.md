The registration said this was the expected outcome and said so before the arms were built: *"This round is expected to produce another null, and that expectation is on the record."* It is the finding, and it is reported as one. No consolation winner is nominated, no bar is softened, nothing was re-parameterized after a result was seen. 5 of the 7 arms DO beat the incumbent on criterion 1, by margins of +0.0037 to +0.0121 Sharpe against intervals of +/-0.064 to +/-0.198 — which is what a mechanism worth +0.0120 looks like when the tightest arm can resolve about +0.06. ## What round 5 was, and why these four arms

Round 4 tested combination RULES on a frozen sleeve and found nothing. Its adversarial counter-agent pass (PASS WITH CORRECTIONS — the null is REAL) found five material errors in the writeup, two of which pointed somewhere round 4 had not looked. Round 5 is those two, plus the two design faults the same pass exposed:

| arm | what it tests | which round-4 finding it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| D1-05 / D1-10 / D1-15 | sleeve idle CAPITAL routed to BIL (cash), not the core, at 5/10/15% | M2 — BIL routing lifts the sleeve's Sharpe with correlation UNCHANGED; the CORE routing round 4 registered as C7 is dominated |
| D2 | the C8 correlation trigger anchored at 15%/5% instead of 30%/10%, on D1's routing | verifier H1 — C8's value was its TIMING, but the trigger was only ever tested at weights M2 says are wrong |
| D3 | a TRUE uncapped inverse-vol sleeve | M5 — round 4's 30% cap bound on 82.1% of days, so C3 was a restatement of C6-monthly and inverse-vol was never tested |
| D4-daily / D4-band005 | D1's best weight under daily and 0.5%-band rebalancing | M3 — a 5% ABSOLUTE band never fires on a 5% target, so round 4 left the rule dimension unregistered |

The registration also fixed the prior: the mechanism M2 identified is worth about **+0.0120 Sharpe** at its tangency weight of 9.8% — five times smaller than the ~+0.06 edge round 4 could resolve at a 5% sleeve, and far inside every interval in the table below. An arm cannot clear a bar that needs its interval to exclude zero on an effect that small.

## Protocol (fixed before results, carried over verbatim from round 4)

- Windows: REAL 2020-12-03..2026-08-19 (n=1433) and SYNTHETIC-EXTENDED 2016-08-23..2026-08-19 (n=2511), identical dates across every comparator within a window.
- Incumbents: INC-B5, INC-3070SPY, INC-SPY. Best incumbent on the full real window by BIL-excess Sharpe: **INC-B5**.
- Measurement basis: daily MTM total returns on adjusted closes; CAGR calendar-annualized at 365.25; Sharpe/Sortino annualized sqrt(252); drawdown peak-to-trough within each window.
- Sortino convention: standard — downside deviation over N. Sharpe and Sortino are printed on BOTH risk-free bases and never mixed within a column.
- Costs 10 bps per side on traded notional. Tail sleeve = TAIL (registered, identical for every variant).
- **Rebalance rule is REGISTERED, not implicit** (the round-4 lesson): monthly calendar for D1, D2 and D3; D4 exists to vary it. No absolute band is used anywhere in this round except as D4's own 0.5% arm.
- Sleeve: FROZEN R2-A dollar curve (data/r2a_daily.json), reused verbatim; daily invested notional from data/r2a_exposure.json.
- Bootstrap: paired circular block bootstrap on the Sharpe DIFFERENCE, 21-day blocks, 4000 draws, seed 20260822, basis BIL-excess daily returns; the Bonferroni tail is read from a 40,000-draw run of the same seeded sequence.
- Survival requires ALL FOUR: 1. Sharpe AND Sortino beat the best incumbent on the full real window; 2. the 95% bootstrap CI on dSharpe excludes zero; 3. it beats the incumbent in >=2 of 3 equal sub-periods; 4. it does not lose the synthetic window by more than 0.10 Sharpe.

D4's weight is the registered selection rule applied once: the D1 arm with the highest BIL-excess Sharpe over the full real window, ties broken toward the lower weight. It selected **10%**.

## Honesty box

- **The prior was registered and it held.** The registration stated before any code existed that D1 and D2 are ADJACENT to arms that already failed, that the expected uplift was ~+0.0120 Sharpe, and that the honest expectation was another null. That is what happened. This is not a vindicated forecast — it is a round that was not worth much power to begin with, run anyway because the mechanism was mechanical rather than searched.
- **Resolution is per arm and this round cannot see its own hypothesis.** Half-widths run from +/-0.064 to +/-0.507; the smallest detectable edge across all arms is +0.06. A +0.0120 mechanism is invisible to it. No arm here is evidence AGAINST the cash-routing mechanism either — failure to detect an effect five times below the detection floor is not a refutation.
- **Multiple comparisons span both rounds.** 22 arms have now been run against the same incumbent on the same 1433 days. Anything claimed as a discovery needs a 99.773% interval to exclude zero. Nothing is close, in either round.
- **In-sample status is unchanged and it is total.** `barbell-lab/config/portfolio.yaml` fixed B.5's weights on 2026-08-11 with no derivation record; the data ends 2026-08-19. The comparator was chosen with hindsight over exactly this window. The R2-A sleeve carries survivorship, universe/alias hindsight and exit-engine overfit risk, and its 30% weight came from an H13 sweep against a SPY core. No CAGR here is a forecast.
- **The idle-capital input is a rebuild, not a measurement of the live book.** `data/r2a_exposure.json` comes from re-running the round-2 pipeline; it matches the frozen curve on all 2,672 days to <1e-6, but it inherits every assumption of that pipeline. D1 and D2 also assume idle cash earns a full day of BIL from the prior close with no execution lag and no transaction cost on the implied daily cash movement — favourable assumptions that make D1 a CEILING on the cash fix, not a floor.
- **The synthetic window is a FLOOR.** Proxy splice, unadjusted, no manager alpha; 1079 of 2511 sessions use proxy returns. Criterion 4 is again not binding, because B.5 alone is the weakest comparator there.
- **What was NOT modeled:** taxes; bid-ask and market impact beyond a flat 10 bps per side; borrow, margin or liquidity constraints; the 20% short-vol bot the config actually pairs B.5 with; PHYS premium/discount; fund-closure or capacity risk; the tail-sleeve question (#1), still open and still held at TAIL.
- **Nothing is promoted.** No config change, no live weight. There are no survivors to nominate; had there been, they would have become HYPOTHESES.md entries at most. Refinements go to a round-6 registration, not into this one.
