## Ambiguity calls (most conservative reading, per contract)

- The 30/70 baseline is the daily-rebalanced return mix 0.3·r_R2A + 0.7·r_SPY on
  the R2-A/SPY intersection calendar, compounded from $100k — the H13 construction;
  its registered CAGR (+15.89%) is trading-day annualized, the tables here use the
  house calendar-day convention (both reproductions asserted).
- R3-A applies the R2-D accrual convention inside the blend: the sleeve return
  becomes r_R2A,t + cash_frac_t·r_BIL,t with cash_frac_t = 1 − the R2-A book's
  invested MTM fraction as of the PRIOR close (Σ (risk$/risk)·price / equity,
  clamped [0,1]) — yesterday's idle cash earns today's BIL return, exactly as R2-D.
- R3-B/R3-D monthly updates: signals from PRIOR-close data at each month's first
  trading day, new weights/state effective from that day's CLOSE (the round-2
  momentum-book execution convention) — one conservative day at the old state.
- R3-B vols = sample stdev of each sleeve's trailing 60 daily returns ending at the
  prior close; <60 returns available → weight stays 0.30; σ_R2A = 0 (all-cash
  stretch) → weight at the 0.45 clamp (vol-scaling sends w→∞; the clamp binds).
- R3-C financing accrues daily on the borrowed fraction at r_BIL,t + 150/252 bps;
  leverage resets daily (no path-dependent margin); L = 1.0 reduces exactly to the
  baseline (asserted). The DD-matched L is descriptive/ex-post only.
- R3-D TSMOM = SPY adjusted close at the last bar BEFORE the rebalance day vs 252
  SPY bars earlier (prior-close 12-month total return); negative → the 70% leg
  earns r_BIL until the next monthly check. SPY history reaches 1993, so the
  lookback never truncates in-window.
- R3-E re-sizes only: the taken set (200dma gate + cap 10, round-2 selection) is
  unchanged; risk fraction per call = 1.25% (rel_strength_60d), 0.75%
  (volume_anomaly), 1.0% otherwise, of the previous close's equity.
- R3-F rebuilds trailing rows per cell with parameterized copies of the round-1/2
  graders (the r1/r2 scripts are untouched; the (3.0, 90) cell is asserted
  row-identical to round 2's build_trailing_rows). Open-at-end rows excluded per
  the round-1 convention; gate/cap/book mechanics identical across cells.
- R3-G resamples the BASELINE's daily returns (stationary bootstrap: geometric
  block lengths mean 21d, wrap-around at the series end, fixed seed), horizon =
  the actual window length; percentiles are empirical (no interpolation).
