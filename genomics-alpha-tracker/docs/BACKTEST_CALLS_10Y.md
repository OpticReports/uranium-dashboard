# 10-Year Calls-Engine Replay — ALL replayable signals, point-in-time catalyst calendar

_Generated by `scripts/backtest_calls_10y.py` · 32 names (22 with PIT catalysts) · 2016-01-01 → 2026-08-19 · adjusted daily bars + point-in-time ClinicalTrials.gov record history (store built 2026-08-20 by `scripts/build_pit_catalysts.py`)_

## Read this first

- **The catalyst-conditioned flags are replayed for the FIRST TIME.** The
  point-in-time calendar is rebuilt from CT.gov's historical record versions:
  for each date d it uses what the registry **had been TOLD as of d**.
  COUNTER-AGENT CORRECTION: interval dates are the sponsor's SUBMIT/QC dates,
  which precede public posting — measured lag across all 6,508 cached
  versions: median 2 days, p90 5 days, max 92 (first records: ~4 days). The
  replay therefore sees updates slightly EARLIER than the market could. The
  bias was measured, not assumed: rebuilding the store on true post dates
  moves quiet_before_catalyst +0.023→+0.045 and pullback_into_catalyst
  +0.011→+0.016 (binary unchanged) — i.e. the look-ahead HURT the catalyst
  flags, and every verdict is identical on either basis.
- **PCD is an ESTIMATE of primary completion, not a readout date.** Data
  often reads out months after (or before) the registered PCD; treat the
  catalyst windows as approximations of event proximity, not event dates.
- **Survivorship, relative-only**: today's universe — dead names absent.
  Absolute expectancies are not forecasts; only flag-vs-baseline and
  flag-vs-flag comparisons are meaningful.
- **Live gates NOT replayed (loud deviation)**: the composite>=55 gate and
  confidence/conviction sizing need hype/analyst/positioning history that
  does not exist. Every suppressed fire becomes a call at FIXED 1R. The live
  auto-trigger set (sentiment ramp / revision cluster / options+social) is
  itself not replayable; the combined book here is the REPLAYABLE set.
- Thresholds are the LIVE pre-registered flags.yaml/calls.yaml values
  (guarded by assertions), NOT fit on this data.
- Entry = the NEXT bar's OPEN after a fire (conservative: a fire computed on
  a close is actionable at the following open). Exits use the production
  `grade_call` — open-first, gap-aware. Slippage: tiered per-side bps
  (A=10/B=40/C=100) re-graded against the original risk unit, as in
  BACKTEST_CALLS.md; `avg R net` is the headline.
- These results are **WALLED OFF from live sizing** (TUNING.md): they set
  observe-only priorities and promotion candidates, never the paper book.

## Deviations from the live engine (read before acting)

- **No composite>=55 gate, no confidence sizing, no 14d cooldown, no Tier-C
  exclusion**: hype/analyst/positioning components have no point-in-time
  history. Fires -> calls 1:1 at fixed 1R (Tier-C names ARE included, with
  100bps/side slippage). Live behavior would trade FEWER, larger calls.
- **The combined book is NOT the live trigger set**: none of the live
  auto-call triggers is replayable; conversely, most replayed flags are
  observe-only live. This measures signal quality, not the live book.
- **PIT calendar granularity**: CT.gov submission lag means a sponsor's
  internal slippage of a readout is invisible until posted — same blindness
  as the live pipeline, so the replay is faithful to what the desk would see.
- Catalyst flags replay ONLY CT.gov trial milestones: no PDUFA/AdComm/
  conference/earnings lanes (operator-curated or other-sourced live), so
  live catalyst flags fire on a superset of these events.
- Bars were fetched via the raw Yahoo chart API in this environment (the
  yfinance cookie flow is blocked here); identical fields/adjustments as the
  provider path.

## Known limitations

- Survivorship (current universe), longs only, no borrow/fees, daily bars.
- Younger names contribute only their listed history — the sample tilts recent.
- Overlapping calls within and across correlated names: `clusters` is the
  honest effective-sample-size guide, and the cluster bootstrap carries the
  dependence — but 10 years still spans only ~3 distinct biotech regimes.
- The per-name trial cap (60, phase-3 first) can drop old phase-2 catalysts
  for the largest sponsors (logged in the PIT build).
- **Alias & universe hindsight**: today's `ctgov_names` aliases (MRNA →
  ModernaTX) and today's universe membership are applied across all 10 years —
  a mild form of hindsight (LLY alone is 16% of quiet_before_catalyst fires,
  and MRNA/BNTX would not have been on the 2016 list).
- 143 fires open at data end are EXCLUDED from call stats; their mark-to-market
  runs ~+0.8R average, so the exclusion is conservative (understates results).
- Edge case: 3 of 278 binary_event calls exited on/after their driving PCD
  (expiry-clamp boundary); immaterial to the table.
- **Fire concentration (post-hoc diagnostic, not a result)**: MRNA/IONS/CERS/
  LLY/BNTX supply ~80% of catalyst-flag fires — perpetual-pipeline sponsors
  where a high-impact PCD is nearly always within the window, so proximity
  stops discriminating. Counter-agent re-attribution: the single-name drag is
  CERS (−17.2R across quiet fires); LLY's quiet calls average ABOVE the full
  sample. Any per-name cap / pipeline-density normalization is a NEW
  hypothesis for the backlog, not a conclusion of this replay.
