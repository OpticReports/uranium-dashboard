## Why nothing survived, and what criterion 4 did not test

The mechanism is not new evidence, it is the round-4 counter-agent's finding restated on a wider grid: on a B.5 core the optimal R2-A weight is a few per cent — effectively zero — because a sleeve helps at the margin only when its Sharpe exceeds rho times the core's, and here 0.358 sits barely above 0.329 x 0.918 = 0.302. Every C1..C8 arm is a way of holding MORE sleeve than that, or of trading it differently, so the whole grid lands below the core alone. Within C1 the damage is monotone in sleeve weight (-0.004 at 5% to -0.013 at 10% to -0.027 at 15% to -0.043 at 20%), and it continues to -0.093 at the live 30% — though that last figure is `C6-band05`, a C6 arm, not a C1 one.

Two numbers for "the optimal weight", because they answer different questions. The closed-form tangency weight on the sleeve AS FROZEN is 3.97% and the costless daily-rebalanced empirical argmax agrees at ~4%, worth +0.0019 Sharpe. But in THIS harness's own implementation — the live 5% band, 10bps a side — Sharpe(BIL) is monotone decreasing from w=0 across the whole 0-30% grid, so the implemented optimum is exactly 0% (0.9185). "About 5%" is the costless figure; the tradable figure is zero.

Criterion 4 was NOT binding. Every arm passed it, because the best incumbent (B.5 alone) is the WEAKEST comparator on the synthetic window — Sharpe(BIL) 0.75 there against 0.89 for 30/70 R2-A/SPY. A "Y" in column 4 means "did not lose to a weak bar", not "held up out of sample". Read columns 1-3 as the test.

### The mechanism, with the accounting artifact repaired (M2)

The arithmetic above runs on S_sleeve = 0.358 — a number DEPRESSED by the very accounting artifact C7 exists to fix, because the frozen sleeve holds dead cash. Repair it two ways and the sleeve's marginal value separates cleanly:

| sleeve treatment | Sharpe(BIL) of the sleeve | rho to core | tangency w* | Sharpe uplift at w* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| as frozen (dead idle cash) | 0.358 | 0.329 | 4.0% | +0.0019 |
| idle capital -> BIL (cash) | 0.443 | 0.329 | 9.8% | +0.0120 |
| idle capital -> CORE (C7 as registered) | 0.645 | 0.626 | 6.6% | +0.0045 |

**Routing idle sleeve cash to the CORE buys Sharpe by buying CORRELATION, and is strictly dominated by routing it to CASH.** Crediting the core lifts the sleeve's own Sharpe from 0.358 to 0.645, but it drags rho from 0.329 to 0.626, so the tangency weight only reaches 6.6% and the uplift is +0.0045. Crediting BIL lifts it to 0.443 while leaving rho untouched at 0.329, roughly doubling the optimal weight to 9.8% for +0.0120. **C7 as registered was the wrong fix.** The right one — idle cash to CASH — was never registered this round and may not be back-fitted into it; it is registered as D1 in [docs/VARIANTS_PREREGISTRATION_R5_SLEEVE.md](VARIANTS_PREREGISTRATION_R5_SLEEVE.md). Note the size: +0.0120 Sharpe is far INSIDE most of the arms' own intervals below, so this is a mechanism, not a promise.

### C3 is VOID AS SPECIFIED (M5)

C3 registered inverse-vol weighting with the sleeve **capped at 30%** as a fixed parameter. Uncapped, the ~20%-vol sleeve against a ~12%-vol core wants a mean weight of 40.3% (median 34.1%, max 93.5%), so the applied weight sits AT the 30% cap on **1177/1433 = 82.1%** of real-window days (864 genuine cap binds plus 313 warm-up / zero-sleeve-vol fallbacks, which also default to 30%). Mean applied weight 0.2954, minimum 0.2390.

C3 is therefore functionally a restatement of C6-monthly — CAGR 12.76% vs 12.75%, maxDD 13.3% vs 13.3%, Sharpe(BIL) 0.8286 vs 0.8258. **The inverse-vol hypothesis was never tested this round.** This is a REGISTRATION flaw, not a harness bug: the harness implements the registration exactly, and the registration fixed a parameter that neutered its own variant. C3's result stands as reported but is VOID AS SPECIFIED as evidence about inverse-vol weighting; the uncapped test is registered as D3 in round 5. Direction is safe either way — uncapped would hold MORE sleeve, which every row in this document says is worse. (On 11 of 65 monthly decisions that HAVE a full 60-day history the trailing sleeve vol is exactly zero — the sleeve is flat for three straight months — and the 30% fallback there coincides with what uncapped inverse-vol would give, so it introduces no distortion.)

## C7 — the mechanical one

C7 is the only arm whose edge, if any, is NOT a search result: it is a fix to a known accounting artifact. **This arm was RE-IMPLEMENTED after the counter-agent pass (M1).** The round-4 draft credited the core's return only on days whose sleeve return was exactly zero (424/1432 = 29.6% of the real window), and justified stopping there with the claim that "the frozen sleeve is a dollar curve, so only FULLY idle days are observable". That claim was FALSE about this repo: `backtest_variants_r2.py`'s `run_call_book_yield` already computes `equity - invested` every day, so the idle balance is exact. `scripts/build_r2a_exposure.py` rebuilds R2-A's book from the round-2 pipeline (601 taken, 2457 skipped at the cap), gates the rebuilt equity against the frozen curve on all 2,672 days, and records the daily invested notional in `data/r2a_exposure.json`.

R2-A's real-window idle CAPITAL: **mean 63.3%, median 57.4%** of the sleeve — with 428/1433 days fully idle and 463/1433 above 90% idle. The draft's fix was therefore about **half** the size of the real artifact. C7 now credits the prior close's idle FRACTION with the core's return each day.

Result: Sharpe(BIL) 0.868 vs INC-B5's 0.918 (dSharpe -0.051, 95% CI [-0.370, +0.250]), Sortino(BIL) 1.255 vs 1.316, CAGR 14.99% vs 14.10%, maxDD 17.1% vs 14.7%. **DOES NOT SURVIVE** the registered bar.

Like-for-like (DESCRIPTIVE, not the registered bar): against the SAME book with the fix off (`C6-band05`), C7 is dSharpe +0.042, 95% CI [-0.059, +0.145], p=0.397. The fix buys +2.22pp of CAGR and costs +3.7pp of max drawdown and +2.0pp of volatility: crediting idle sleeve cash to the core raises the return AND the equity-beta of the sleeve leg together, so Sharpe rises (0.825 -> 0.868) while Calmar FALLS (0.95 -> 0.87) and the interval on the Sharpe move still spans zero. The accounting artifact was real and is now fixed at its true size; correcting it does NOT lift the book past the incumbent, and the mechanism section shows why this routing is the dominated one.

Correction size, before -> after: the draft's zero-return-days implementation gave Sharpe(BIL) 0.828, dSharpe -0.090, CI [-0.449, +0.250], CAGR 13.63%, maxDD 15.5%. The idle-CAPITAL implementation gives 0.868, -0.051, [-0.370, +0.250], 14.99%, 17.1%. Better, and still not close: it fails criteria 1, 2 and 3 (0/3 sub-periods). **The null survives the correction.** See the mechanism section for why the registered routing was the wrong one: crediting the CORE is dominated by crediting CASH.

Implementation limits that remain, stated because they bound the claim: the idle fraction is measured at the PRIOR close and earns a full day of the routed leg's return, and no transaction cost is charged for the implied daily movement of idle cash in and out of the core — both favourable assumptions. The sleeve's own trade slippage stays as the round-2 engine charged it.

## Honesty box

- **In-sample status of B.5's weights.** `barbell-lab/config/portfolio.yaml` was first committed **2026-08-11**; the data here ends **2026-08-19**. Effectively 100% of both windows precedes the moment the weights were fixed, and there is no derivation record in the repo — no optimizer run, no pre-registration, no fitting log. The weights were chosen by someone who already knew which assets won 2021-2026. No CAGR in this document is a forecast.
- **The synthetic window is a FLOOR, not a point estimate.** The splice uses the proxies' own unadjusted returns and adds no manager alpha; AVUV beat SLYV by ~5.1pp/yr and AVDV beat DLS by ~6.7pp/yr over their measurable overlaps (geometric), while KMLM's proxies BEAT KMLM by 1.3-3.1pp/yr. 1079 of 2511 synthetic sessions use proxy returns for 54% of B.5's weight plus 14% KMLM and 5% TAIL.
- **R2-A's inherited caveats propagate into every blend number here.** The sleeve is a backtest carrying survivorship ("today's universe; dead names absent — absolute numbers flattered"), universe/alias hindsight, and exit-engine overfit risk; the 30% weight was itself selected by an H13 sweep run against a **SPY** core on a backtested sleeve. None of that is corrected here.
- **Config mismatch.** `portfolio.yaml` specifies B.5 at **80% of the book paired with a 20% short-vol bot** under a hard constraint `bot_corr_max: 0.10`. Every variant here instead pairs B.5 with R2-A, whose real-window correlation to the B.5 core is **0.329** — 3.3x that gate. Whether R2-A is meant to occupy the bot slot, or be a third sleeve, is UNRESOLVED and was not adjudicated by this round.
- **The tail sleeve is an unresolved input, held FIXED at TAIL.** `portfolio.yaml` marks it a placeholder pending Question #1 with candidates [TAIL, CAOS, BTAL, NONE]. TAIL is the worst of the four in the real window (a 133bp CAGR span, 0.04 Sharpe), so these are the pessimistic corner. The registration fixes TAIL for every variant so the open question cannot flatter one over another — it does NOT resolve the question.
- **Resolution is PER ARM; there is no campaign-wide noise floor.** The paired 95% CI half-width runs from +/-0.056 (C1-05) to +/-0.403 (the 30%-sleeve arms) — a 7x span. An earlier draft of this document quoted a single "+/-0.4" figure imported from the B.5-core vs SPY-core comparison in a different study; that was wrong, and it made the round look blinder than it is. The injected-edge table above is the honest statement: at low sleeve weight this campaign resolved a true +0.10 Sharpe edge and found none; at the live 30% weight it could not have seen +0.40.
- **Two of the eight registered variants did not test what they claimed.** C3's fixed 30% cap made it a restatement of C6-monthly (VOID AS SPECIFIED for inverse-vol); C7's registered routing of idle cash to the CORE is dominated by routing it to cash, so the arm tested the wrong fix even after being re-implemented correctly. Both are registration flaws found by the counter-agent, not harness bugs, and neither may be re-parameterized inside this round.
- **Criterion 1's failures are not all robust.** C1-05 passes criterion 1 under daily, 0.5%-band and monthly rebalancing and fails it under the live 5% absolute band, which never fires at a 5% target. Criteria 2 and 3 carry the null: they fail under every reading tried.
- **Independent reproduction.** An adversarial counter-agent re-ran the full campaign (byte-identical report), rebuilt the R2-A book from the round-2 pipeline and matched the FROZEN curve on all **2,672 days to <1e-6**, reimplemented the paired block bootstrap naively and matched this harness's optimized version to **5.3e-15** on every draw, ran a 200-run coverage simulation (96.0% empirical coverage), and re-ran the whole bar on the rf=0 basis, against the strongest synthetic-window incumbent, and under the prior round's stationary-bootstrap convention. No verdict moved. Verdict: PASS WITH CORRECTIONS — the null is REAL.
- **What was NOT modeled:** taxes; bid-ask and market impact beyond a flat 10bps per side; borrow, margin or liquidity constraints; the 20% short-vol bot the config actually pairs B.5 with; PHYS premium/discount as a separate factor; fund-closure or capacity risk. C4's deleveraging assumes cash earns BIL with no execution lag.
- **Warm-up rules, disclosed because they are choices.** C3 and C8 need 60 trailing days and C4 needs 20; before that history exists inside a window their weight falls back to the live 30% (C3/C8) or to full exposure (C4). That is a stated rule, not a fitted parameter.
