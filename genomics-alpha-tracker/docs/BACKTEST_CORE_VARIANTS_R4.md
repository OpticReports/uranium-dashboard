## Protocol (fixed before results)

- Windows, identical dates across every comparator within a window: REAL 2020-12-03..2026-08-19 (n=1433) and SYNTHETIC-EXTENDED 2016-08-23..2026-08-19 (n=2511).
- Incumbents: INC-B5, INC-3070SPY, INC-SPY. Best incumbent on the full real window by BIL-excess Sharpe: **INC-B5**.
- Measurement basis: daily MTM total returns on adjusted closes; CAGR calendar-annualized at 365.25; Sharpe/Sortino annualized sqrt(252); drawdown peak-to-trough within each window.
- Sortino convention: standard — downside deviation over N — the round-4 counter-agent flagged the earlier code for dividing by the count of negative days instead. Sharpe and Sortino are printed on BOTH risk-free bases and never mixed within a column.
- Costs 10 bps per side on traded notional. Tail sleeve = TAIL (registered, identical for every variant).
- Sleeve: FROZEN R2-A dollar curve (data/r2a_daily.json), reused verbatim. Synthetic KMLM: KMLM spliced to DBMF then WTMF, unadjusted.
- Bootstrap: paired circular block bootstrap on the Sharpe DIFFERENCE, 21-day blocks, 4000 draws, seed 20260822, basis BIL-excess daily returns. Both series are resampled with the SAME block starts, so the difference keeps its date pairing.
- XBI 200dma prior-close gate source (C5): backtest_bars.json (adj_close).

## Why nothing survived, and what criterion 4 did not test

The mechanism is not new evidence, it is the round-4 counter-agent's finding restated on a wider grid: on a B.5 core the optimal R2-A weight is about 5% — effectively zero — because a sleeve helps at the margin only when its Sharpe exceeds rho times the core's, and here 0.358 sits barely above 0.329 x 0.918 = 0.302. Every C1..C8 arm is a way of holding MORE sleeve than that, or of trading it differently, so the whole grid lands below the core alone. Within C1 the damage is monotone in sleeve weight (-0.004 at 5% to -0.043 at 20% to -0.093 at the live 30%), and every CI is far wider than its own gap: this round found no edge AND could not have resolved one this small if it existed.

Criterion 4 was NOT binding. Every arm passed it, because the best incumbent (B.5 alone) is the WEAKEST comparator on the synthetic window — Sharpe(BIL) 0.75 there against 0.89 for 30/70 R2-A/SPY. A "Y" in column 4 means "did not lose to a weak bar", not "held up out of sample". Read columns 1-3 as the test.

## C7 — the mechanical one

C7 is the only arm whose edge, if any, is NOT a search result: it is a fix to a known accounting artifact. The frozen R2-A sleeve earns 0% on idle cash on **424/1432 = 29.6%** of real-window days (the counter-agent's own measurement, reproduced here); C7 routes that idle cash to the CORE's return instead.

Result: Sharpe(BIL) 0.828 vs INC-B5's 0.918 (dSharpe -0.090, 95% CI [-0.449, +0.250]), Sortino(BIL) 1.200 vs 1.316, CAGR 13.63% vs 14.10%, maxDD 15.5% vs 14.7%. **DOES NOT SURVIVE** the registered bar.

Like-for-like (DESCRIPTIVE, not the registered bar): against the SAME book with the fix off (`C6-band05`), C7 is dSharpe +0.003, 95% CI [-0.108, +0.114], p=0.937. The fix buys +0.86pp of CAGR and costs +2.0pp of max drawdown and +1.0pp of volatility: crediting idle sleeve cash to the core raises both the return and the equity-beta of the sleeve leg, so the Sharpe barely moves (0.825 -> 0.828) and Calmar FALLS (0.95 -> 0.88). The accounting artifact was real and is now fixed; it was not hiding a risk-adjusted edge.

Implementation limit, stated because it bounds the claim: the frozen sleeve is a dollar curve, so only FULLY idle days (exactly 0% return) are observable. Partially-invested days keep 0% on their cash, so C7 as run is the conservative corner of the fix. It also charges no transaction cost for the implied daily movement of idle cash in and out of the core, which is a favourable assumption.

## Honesty box

- **In-sample status of B.5's weights.** `barbell-lab/config/portfolio.yaml` was first committed **2026-08-11**; the data here ends **2026-08-19**. Effectively 100% of both windows precedes the moment the weights were fixed, and there is no derivation record in the repo — no optimizer run, no pre-registration, no fitting log. The weights were chosen by someone who already knew which assets won 2021-2026. No CAGR in this document is a forecast.
- **The synthetic window is a FLOOR, not a point estimate.** The splice uses the proxies' own unadjusted returns and adds no manager alpha; AVUV beat SLYV by ~5.1pp/yr and AVDV beat DLS by ~6.7pp/yr over their measurable overlaps (geometric), while KMLM's proxies BEAT KMLM by 1.3-3.1pp/yr. 1079 of 2511 synthetic sessions use proxy returns for 54% of B.5's weight plus 14% KMLM and 5% TAIL.
- **R2-A's inherited caveats propagate into every blend number here.** The sleeve is a backtest carrying survivorship ("today's universe; dead names absent — absolute numbers flattered"), universe/alias hindsight, and exit-engine overfit risk; the 30% weight was itself selected by an H13 sweep run against a **SPY** core on a backtested sleeve. None of that is corrected here.
- **Config mismatch.** `portfolio.yaml` specifies B.5 at **80% of the book paired with a 20% short-vol bot** under a hard constraint `bot_corr_max: 0.10`. Every variant here instead pairs B.5 with R2-A, whose real-window correlation to the B.5 core is **0.329** — 3.3x that gate. Whether R2-A is meant to occupy the bot slot, or be a third sleeve, is UNRESOLVED and was not adjudicated by this round.
- **The tail sleeve is an unresolved input, held FIXED at TAIL.** `portfolio.yaml` marks it a placeholder pending Question #1 with candidates [TAIL, CAOS, BTAL, NONE]. TAIL is the worst of the four in the real window (a 133bp CAGR span, 0.04 Sharpe), so these are the pessimistic corner. The registration fixes TAIL for every variant so the open question cannot flatter one over another — it does NOT resolve the question.
- **Noise floor.** The counter-agent measured the 95% CI on a Sharpe difference in this data at roughly +/-0.4. With ~1,400 real trading days, differences smaller than that are not distinguishable from zero, and the CI column above is the honest reading of every dSharpe here.
- **What was NOT modeled:** taxes; bid-ask and market impact beyond a flat 10bps per side; borrow, margin or liquidity constraints; the 20% short-vol bot the config actually pairs B.5 with; PHYS premium/discount as a separate factor; fund-closure or capacity risk. C4's deleveraging assumes cash earns BIL with no execution lag.
- **Warm-up rules, disclosed because they are choices.** C3 and C8 need 60 trailing days and C4 needs 20; before that history exists inside a window their weight falls back to the live 30% (C3/C8) or to full exposure (C4). That is a stated rule, not a fitted parameter.
