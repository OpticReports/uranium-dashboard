## Read this first

Inherits EVERY caveat of BACKTEST_CALLS_10Y.md — one line each:

- **Survivorship**: today's universe; dead names absent — absolute numbers flattered.
- **Alias/universe hindsight**: today's aliases and membership applied across all 10 years.
- **PIT submit-date lag**: catalyst knowledge at CT.gov posting granularity.
- **No composite/confidence gates**: every suppressed fire trades at fixed fraction —
  the live engine would trade fewer, larger calls.
- **Unreplayable live triggers**: sentiment/revision/options lanes absent; this is the
  replayable shadow, not the live book.

Campaign-specific caveats:

- **11 variants = multiple comparisons**: one full-period winner alone is noise;
  the pre-registered bar (Sharpe AND Calmar, full period AND ≥2/3 sub-periods) is the test.
- **V10 is EXPLORATORY** (different exit engine, highest overfit risk — flagged in the
  contract; a survival here is a hypothesis to re-derive, not a result).
- **V6 is a different engine shape**: monthly rebalance, always-in, no stops — its
  Sharpe/Calmar are not like-for-like with call books, though the bar is applied anyway;
  V6a additionally carries the warnings in its own block below the table.
- **Hedging costs nothing here**: V3/V4's XBI shorts carry no borrow/fees/slippage
  (deep ETF, but not free in practice) — treat hedged results as upper bounds.
- **rf = 0 understates the gated variants**: V1/V2/V6b sit in cash for large fractions
  of the decade and real cash earns T-bills (~2%+ avg 2016–2026) — the omission's
  direction FAVORS them in reality.
- **XBI buy-and-hold itself clears the survival bar vs V0**: "survives" means
  "better than a strategy that was no better than its own sector ETF" — this
  campaign is fixing a broken baseline, not certifying alpha.
- **Survivors are HYPOTHESES** (TUNING.md law): never direct config changes.

## Protocol

$100k start · risk 1% of the previous close's equity per call (V9 scales this; V6
is fully invested) · daily MTM on adjusted closes ((px−entry)/risk × risk$) ·
realized r_net (tiered slippage A=10/B=40/C=100 bps per side) on the exit date ·
rf = 0 · Sharpe/Sortino annualized √252 · sub-period stats on that segment of the
same curve, CAGR annualized on segment length. Machinery check: V0 reproduces the
BACKTEST_CALLS_10Y.md addendum ($208,764 / +7.19% / 65.5% / 0.42) within ±1%.

**SURVIVES** = Sharpe AND Calmar beat V0 over the full period AND in ≥2 of 3
sub-periods (2016-2019, 2020-2022, 2023-2026Aug).

### V6a — do NOT carry the headline number without these

- **82.4% max drawdown is untradeable as a standalone book.** No
  mandate survives it; the row is an engine-shape comparison, not a candidate book.
- **5-name equal weight is extreme idiosyncratic concentration**: each pick is a
  binary-event lottery ticket at 20% of the book, drawn from a 24-name eligible
  universe.
- **Cross-sectional momentum on a survivor-only universe is the WORST CASE of the
  inherited survivorship bias**: "buy the recent winners among names known today to
  have survived and mattered" — the blown-up names momentum would have chased into
  zero (the strategy's actual tail) are absent by construction. **V6a's absolute
  CAGR is unusable.** The ONLY reading that survives is the RELATIVE one: momentum
  ranking adds selection value INSIDE this universe.
- Robustness (counter-agent reruns): the plumbing is NOT manufacturing the result —
  no tier filter (all 32 names) ends at $2,177,355 (BETTER: the hindsight tier
  filter cost ~3pp CAGR); tier-true costs (B=40 bps/side) end at $1,529,190
  (−1.3pp CAGR, still clears the bar); a 1-day execution delay also survives.
  The universe and the drawdown are the problem, not the tier filter or the costs.

## Ambiguity calls (most conservative reading, per contract)

- Sizing equity reference = the PREVIOUS close's equity (reproduces the addendum
  book to $4; a same-close reference would embed a look-ahead).
- V3/V4 hedge notional fixed at entry (never rebalanced), opened/closed at the
  entry/exit-date XBI closes, zero costs (noted above as an upper bound).
- V7 window = the symbol's own last 5 trading days ending at fire_date; the fire
  record is the replay's graded call rows (fires still open at data end are absent).
- V9 vol = sample stdev of the book's trailing 20 daily returns × √252; the scaled
  fraction applies to entries from day 21 on (first 20 trading days at 1%).
- V10 trail uses peak close AND ATR14 through the PRIOR bar (the level actionable
  at today's open — recomputed daily, per the registered 'trailing' reading); the
  trail activates the bar after entry; initial risk unit = the stored build_levels
  risk (3×ATR14 with the 0.5×entry cap); fires still open at data end excluded,
  matching the replay's convention.
- V6 turnover cost computed on pre-cost target turnover, then post-cost equity
  split equally; a name with no bar within 7 days of a rebalance is excluded that
  month; if fewer than 5 names qualify, the qualifiers are equal-weighted.
- Regime gates (V1/V2/V4/V5/V10 entries; V6b rebalances) test the PRIOR trading
  day's close vs the prior-day SMA — the level actionable at the fill. RESTATED:
  the first run gated on the gate day's own close, a same-day look-ahead the
  counter-agent caught (its rerun: no survival flips; magnitudes restated here).
  An undefined SMA drops the entry (never binds: XBI has full history).
