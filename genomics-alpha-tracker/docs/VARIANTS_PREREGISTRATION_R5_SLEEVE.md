# Variant campaign — ROUND 5 (sleeve mechanics) pre-registration, 2026-08-22

Registered BEFORE any round-5 variant is written or run. Motivated ONLY by
evidence that existed beforehand: the round-4 campaign
([docs/BACKTEST_CORE_VARIANTS_R4.md](BACKTEST_CORE_VARIANTS_R4.md)) and the
adversarial counter-agent verdict on it
(`scratchpad/r4_counter_verdict.md`, PASS WITH CORRECTIONS — the null is
REAL). Every arm below cites the specific finding it comes from. Nothing here
comes from a leaderboard, a sweep, or "C-something looked almost good".

## Why this round exists

Round 4 tested COMBINATION RULES on a frozen sleeve and found nothing: on a
B.5 core, R2-A's tangency weight is ~4% and the tradable optimum is 0%,
because a sleeve pays at the margin only when its Sharpe exceeds rho times the
core's, and 0.358 sits barely above 0.329 x 0.918 = 0.302.

The counter-agent's corrections changed WHERE the remaining lever is. Two
findings, and only two, point somewhere round 4 did not look:

- **M2.** The sleeve's 0.358 Sharpe is depressed by dead idle cash — 63.3% of
  its capital on average. Routing that cash to BIL lifts S_sleeve to 0.443
  with rho UNCHANGED at 0.329, moving w* to 9.8% (uplift +0.0120). Routing it
  to the CORE, which is what round 4 registered as C7, lifts S_sleeve further
  (0.645) but drags rho to 0.626, so w* only reaches 6.6% (uplift +0.0045).
  **Round 4 tested the dominated version of its own mechanical fix.**
- **M5.** C3's registered 30% cap sat at the cap on 82.1% of days, so C3 was a
  restatement of C6-monthly. **Inverse-vol was never tested.**

Two further findings shape the DESIGN rather than adding a hypothesis:

- **M3.** A 5% absolute band never fires on a 5% target weight (0 rebalances
  in 1433 days), so round 4's low-weight arms were buy-and-hold by accident.
  This round fixes an explicit rebalance rule for every arm and registers the
  rule dimension as its own variant instead of leaving it implicit.
- **M4.** Resolution is per arm, not campaign-wide: +/-0.056 at a 5% sleeve,
  +/-0.384 at 30%. Low-weight arms are the ones this data can actually
  adjudicate, which is why every arm here sits at or below a 15% sleeve.

## The honest prior, stated before any result

**This round is expected to produce another null, and that expectation is on
the record.**

- D1 and D2 are ADJACENT to arms that already failed. D1 is C7's routing
  changed from CORE to BIL and its weight lowered; D2 is C8's trigger moved to
  a lower pair of weights. Adjacency to a failure is weak grounds for a
  success.
- The prior expected uplift is the one M2 computed: **~+0.012 Sharpe** at the
  tangency weight. That is far INSIDE several round-4 arms' own 95% intervals
  and inside the +/-0.056 interval of even the tightest arm. A mechanism worth
  +0.012 cannot clear a bar that needs an interval to exclude zero unless the
  interval is much tighter than anything measured so far.
- The smallest edge round 4 could resolve at a 5% sleeve was ~+0.06 Sharpe.
  **+0.012 is five times smaller than that.** Criterion 2 is therefore
  expected to fail on every arm here even if the mechanism is completely real.
- Nothing in this round may be promoted, and a near-miss is not a finding.
  If nothing survives, that IS the finding and it gets reported as such.

## Judgment protocol (fixed BEFORE results — carried over VERBATIM from round 4)

- Two windows, both reported for every variant, never mixed:
  REAL 2020-12-03..2026-08-19 (all B.5 constituents genuinely trading) and
  SYNTHETIC-EXTENDED 2016-08-23..2026-08-19 (proxy splice, unadjusted).
- Metrics: CAGR, max DD, vol, Sharpe (rf=0 AND BIL-excess, both printed),
  Sortino (standard convention: downside deviation over N, not over the
  count of negative days), Calmar, corr to SPY.
- INCUMBENTS: (a) B.5 alone, (b) 30/70 R2-A/SPY, (c) SPY alone.
- A variant SURVIVES only if ALL of:
  1. Sharpe AND Sortino beat the best incumbent over the FULL real window;
  2. the block-bootstrap 95% CI on the Sharpe DIFFERENCE vs that incumbent
     EXCLUDES ZERO (21d blocks, 4000 draws, paired on dates);
  3. it beats that incumbent in >=2 of 3 equal sub-periods of the real window;
  4. it does not LOSE on the synthetic window by more than 0.10 Sharpe.
- Bonferroni-style caution is REQUIRED in the writeup: with N variants the
  naive 5% threshold is wrong; report the count and the implied bar. **This
  round's count must include ROUND 4's arms as well as round 5's**, because
  round 5's hypotheses were generated by looking at round 4's results.
- NO variant may be added, dropped, or re-parameterized after any result is
  seen. Refinements go to a round-6 registration. Nothing here becomes a
  config change or a live weight without a separate promotion (TUNING.md).
- If NOTHING survives, that IS the finding and it gets reported as such.

## Registered variants

Sleeve = R2-A (frozen dollar curve, `data/r2a_daily.json`) with its daily
invested notional from `data/r2a_exposure.json`. Core = B.5 Enhanced. Tail
sleeve held at TAIL for every variant (the conservative corner) so the
unresolved Question #1 cannot flatter one variant over another.

**Rebalance rule, fixed here and not left to the harness (M3):** every arm
below rebalances on the MONTHLY CALENDAR (first trading day of each month),
except D4, whose whole purpose is to vary it. A 5% absolute band is NOT used
anywhere in this round.

- **D1** sleeve idle cash routed to **BIL** (cash), not to the core, at static
  sleeve weights **5 / 10 / 15 %**, monthly rebalance. *(3 arms: D1-05,
  D1-10, D1-15.)*
  Motivation: M2 — routing idle capital to BIL lifts S_sleeve 0.358 -> 0.443
  with rho unchanged at 0.329, giving w* ~= 9.8%. The three weights bracket
  that w* and stay inside the range this data can resolve (M4).
- **D2** D1's routing combined with the correlation trigger evaluated NEAR THE
  TANGENCY WEIGHT rather than at 30/10: sleeve **15% when trailing 60d
  corr(sleeve, core) < 0.30, else 5%**, evaluated monthly. *(1 arm.)*
  Motivation: the counter-agent's hypothesis-generation note H1 — C8's value
  was in its TIMING, not its weight level. Against a static sleeve of the same
  average exposure (18.49%) C8 was +0.036 dSharpe, Sortino 1.320 vs 1.263,
  Calmar 1.012 vs 0.945, and C8 was the only round-4 arm with Calmar > 1 and
  the only one beating INC-B5 on Sortino(BIL). That trigger was only ever
  tested at 30%/10%; this is the same trigger anchored at the weights M2 says
  are correct.
- **D3** a TRUE **uncapped** inverse-vol sleeve: weight = (1/vol_sleeve) /
  (1/vol_sleeve + 1/vol_core) from trailing 60d realized vol, evaluated
  monthly, **no cap**, sleeve idle cash left as frozen (so D3 tests the
  weighting rule, not the cash fix). Its realized weight distribution
  (mean / median / min / max / decile spread) MUST be reported. *(1 arm.)*
  Motivation: M5 — the registered 30% cap in round 4's C3 bound on 82.1% of
  days and made C3 a restatement of C6-monthly, so the inverse-vol hypothesis
  is untested. Warm-up and zero-sleeve-vol months fall back to the round-4
  convention (the live 30%), disclosed as a rule, not a fitted parameter.
- **D4** the REBALANCE-RULE dimension M3 exposed, applied to D1 at its best
  weight: **daily** and **0.5% absolute band**. *(2 arms; the monthly reading
  IS the D1 arm itself and is reported beside them without being counted
  twice.)*
  "Best weight" is fixed here as: the D1 arm with the highest BIL-excess
  Sharpe over the FULL real window, ties broken toward the LOWER weight.
  Motivation: M3 — a 5% absolute band never fires at a 5% target, so round 4's
  criterion-1 results at low weight were an artifact of an unregistered
  implementation choice rather than a property of the strategy.

**Total: 7 registered arms.** Combined with round 4's 15, the
multiple-comparison bar for anything claimed as a discovery in this round is
**22 arms**: naive alpha 0.05, Bonferroni-adjusted alpha 0.05/22 = 0.00227,
i.e. a 99.773% CI must exclude zero.

## Fixed parameters (not tunable this round)

60d vol/correlation lookback; 0.30 correlation threshold; D2's 15%/5% pair;
D1's 5/10/15% grid; monthly calendar rebalance (D4 excepted); 10 bps per
side; TAIL as the tail sleeve; BIL as the cash leg; bootstrap 21d blocks,
4000 draws, seed 20260822; adjudication on BIL-excess Sharpe with rf=0
printed beside it. If any arm looks "almost good", the ONLY allowed action is
registering a round-6 variant.
