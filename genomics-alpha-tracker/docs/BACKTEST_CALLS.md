# Trade-Call Exit-Mechanics Backtest

_Generated by `scripts/backtest_calls.py` · data through 2026-07-14 · 30 names, ~2y adjusted daily bars (FMP)_

## What this is — and is not

This backtests the **exit mechanics** of the calls engine (stop width x ATR14,
reward:risk, time-stop) with **systematic, non-overlapping entries** (every 5th
trading day when flat) using the *exact* live level/grading code, gap-aware fills
included. Because entries are identical across configs, comparisons between
configs are **paired** and meaningful.

**It does NOT validate the signal.** No historical flag/social/revision data
exists yet; signal validation accrues live via `/flags/track-record`. And because
the sample is the *current* universe (survivorship — today's dead names are
absent), **absolute expectancy numbers are not meaningful — only relative
comparisons between exit configs are.** Do not quote the avg-R of any cell as an
expected live return.

## Selection method: plateau on NET R, not argmax

The pick maximizes the **worst slippage-adjusted avg-R in its parameter
neighborhood** (adjacent stop and R:R cells at the same time-stop). The argmax of
36 noisy cells is noise; a config whose neighbors agree is evidence of a real
plateau — and selecting on gross R would reward exactly the tight-stop configs
that trading costs punish hardest.

**Pick: stop 3.0xATR14 · 2.0:1 reward:risk · 45d time-stop**

If the sign of avg-R flips across regimes, treat the pick as regime-conditional —
the honest default is the one that is robust in both, and the regime strip on the
Today tab tells you which regime you are in.

### Slippage sensitivity for the pick (per-side, by liquidity tier)

Universe tiers: {'A': 15, 'B': 13, 'C': 2}. Slippage model: A=10bps, B=40bps, C=100bps per side.

- avg R without slippage: **0.214**
- avg R with tiered slippage: **0.185** (n=515)

If a comparison you care about flips sign under slippage, that is a finding —
size down or skip thin names, which is why Tier C is excluded from auto-calls.

## Known limitations (read before quoting any number)

- **Survivorship**: current-universe bars only; blown-up names are absent.
- **No signal selection**: systematic entries ≠ flag-triggered entries.
- **~2 years = roughly one biotech regime**; the regime split is thin.
- **Longs only**, no borrow/fees, daily bars (no intraday stop dynamics).
- Overlap across names remains (32 correlated names) — hence cluster bootstrap,
  and `clusters` is the honest effective-sample-size guide, not `n`.
