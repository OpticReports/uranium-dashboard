## Read this first

- **Survivorship**: today's universe only — names that died are absent, so
  ONLY relative readings (signal vs baseline, signal vs signal) mean anything.
- **This is the replayable HALF of the engine.** Social/hype, catalyst and
  analyst-revision signals cannot be replayed (no point-in-time history) —
  they earn their record live. The pullback signal here is its PRICE half
  only; the live flag also requires a nearby catalyst.
- Thresholds are the LIVE config values (pre-registered, not fit here).
- Short interest fires 10 trading days after settlement (publication lag).
- These results are WALLED OFF from live sizing (TUNING.md): they set
  observe-only priorities, never the paper book.
