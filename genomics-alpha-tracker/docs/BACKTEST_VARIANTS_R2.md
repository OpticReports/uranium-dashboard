## Protocol

$100k start · risk 1% of the previous close's equity per call (momentum books fully
invested) · daily MTM on adjusted closes · realized r_net (tiered slippage
A=10/B=40/C=100 bps per side) on the exit date · momentum books: monthly
first-trading-day rebalance, trailing 60-bar return vs XBI, equal weight, 10 bps/side
turnover cost, 7-day bar-staleness exclusion · ALL regime gates test the PRIOR
trading day's close vs prior-day SMA (round-1 counter-agent convention, mandatory) ·
rf = 0 except R2-D · Sharpe/Sortino annualized √252 · sub-period stats on that
segment of the same curve. Machinery checks: this engine reproduces the stored
restated V2 and V6b before any round-2 variant is graded, and the R2-D engine at
zero yield reduces exactly to the round-1 engine.

**SURVIVES** = Sharpe AND Calmar beat BOTH baselines (V0 AND restated V2) over the
full period AND in ≥2 of 3 sub-periods (both baselines beaten in the same
sub-period). R2-D: n/a (realism restatement of round-1 survivors).

## Ambiguity calls (most conservative reading, per contract)

- ALL regime gates (R2-A entries; R2-B/C/E and R2-D-V6b rebalances) test the PRIOR
  trading day's close vs the prior-day SMA — the round-1 counter-agent convention,
  applied everywhere; an undefined SMA drops the entry/rebalance (never binds).
- R2-A re-grades EVERY live-flag row with the round-1 V10 mechanics unchanged
  (trail = prior-bar peak close − 3.0×ATR14 through the prior bar, recomputed daily;
  open-first gap fills; 90 CALENDAR-day time stop; initial risk unit = the stored
  build_levels risk; tiered slippage). Rows still open at data end are excluded
  (round-1 convention); duplicate same-day fires from different flags remain
  distinct calls at the cap, exactly as in V0.
- R2-C's PIT floor = median of (adjusted close × volume) over the 60 bars ending at
  the PRIOR bar (knowable before the rebalance; the repo's median_addv dollar-volume
  convention) ≥ $2M; a name failing the floor or lacking 60 bars is excluded that
  month; NO tier filter — the whole 32-name universe is eligible.
- Momentum ranking still uses the rebalance day's own close (round-1 disclosed
  convention; the round-1 counter-agent showed a 1-day delay does not drive results);
  only GATES are prior-close.
- R2-D accrues yesterday's idle cash at today's BIL total return: for call books,
  idle = equity − Σ(risk$/risk × price) (position notional MTM), clamped to
  [0, equity]; for the momentum book, idle = the tracked cash balance (full equity
  when the gate is off). Yield feeds back into sizing (1% of the higher equity).
- BIL daily total return from stockanalysis.com adjusted closes (field 'a'),
  cached at data/bil_bars_raw.json; 0 calendar days lacked a BIL
  bar in-window, so the flat 2.0%/yr proxy was never used.
- Baselines for the double bar are the STORED restated round-1 stats
  (backtest_variants_10y_results.json), not recomputations; the round-2 engine's
  reproduction of V2 and V6b is asserted before grading.
