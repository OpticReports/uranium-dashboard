A Practical Solution for Today's Infrastructure
The world's demand for computing power continues to accelerate while organizations
face increasing challenges in expanding existing data center capacity. New facilities
often require significant capital investment, additional electrical infrastructure, lengthy
permitting processes, extended construction timelines, and increasing community
concerns surrounding large-scale AI data center development.

The CADY™ Compute Accelerator was created to address a different opportunity.

Rather than replacing existing infrastructure or waiting years to build new facilities, our
mission is to help organizations improve the performance and value of the data centers
they already own. By integrating intelligent hardware acceleration into existing
compute environments, organizations can evaluate opportunities to increase
infrastructure performance, improve operational efficiency, extend infrastructure life,
and delay or reduce the need for costly infrastructure expansion.

The future of computing is not simply building more infrastructure. It is making today's
infrastructure significantly more intelligent.
