## Strongest point in the company's favour that the sweeps underweighted

**The Milestone limits its own claims and offers to be checked.**
- It states that the properties were supplied, not perceived.
- It says the reasoning is "not a neural-net-style learned abstraction".
- It offers "a verifiable appendix" containing the taxonomy, rules, classifier logic and exact outputs.

On a careful reading (item 8), its "untouched core" language means *unmodified*, not *unused*. The decisive question, whether the FPGA does the reasoning, therefore **has not yet been asked**, and the company has volunteered evidence that would answer it. Per the house rule (missing inputs: ask, don't analyze around them), the appendix plus a host-kill / bitstream-swap demo should be requested before scoring.

The current record supports **"unproven"**, not **"absent"**.

The runner-up point: Hanson is a verified, currently serving bank CEO with 20 years of bank-partner distribution. That is the moat the IP steelman identifies, and the corporate sweep used it only as a count against him.
