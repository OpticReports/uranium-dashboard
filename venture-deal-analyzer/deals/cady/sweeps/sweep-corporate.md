## (a) Executive read (8 lines)

1. **ICH is a real company, but it is a loyalty-rewards processor, not an AI or chip company.** It is a Nevada corporation formed in 2019, run from a shared office in Newhall, CA, and controlled by the Stewart family through Source Clearinghouse Inc. (68%). Its website, republished 2026-07-04, says nothing about CADY, FPGAs or AI. The CADY "apply" link (/team-3-1) goes to a loyalty **"Strategic Partners"** page that has only a generic contact form. [site](https://www.internationalclearinghouse.com/team-3-1), [sitemap](https://www.internationalclearinghouse.com/pages-sitemap.xml)
2. **The same family has raised private money for one loyalty concept for 25 years.** Source filed paper Form Ds in 2001 and 2006. In 2014 Source raised $4.6M from 82 investors. A $50M 506(c) offering ran 2015–19 and sold $3.05M. Help International filed an S-1 in 2015–16 that never went effective. WeSave raised $500k under Reg D, filed a Reg CF offering, and got a $74.25M Reg A Tier 2 offering qualified on 2024-07-08. WeSave's FY2023 revenue was **$939**. (EDGAR links in §d)
3. **Since the 2024 qualification, WeSave's required SEC reports are missing and both companies show "Default" in Nevada.** No 1-K, 1-SA, 253(g)(2) or 1-Z filing appears for WeSave, and there is no final C-U for its crowdfunding round. ICH and WeSave both show "Default" status in Nevada (OpenCorporates mirror). The loyalty business looks like it was lapsing just before CADY appeared in June 2026.
4. **The other side of the record.** The people are real and connected:
   - Brad Hanson is the ex-CEO of MetaBank (now Pathward). MoneyLion's 10-K independently lists him as a director of Source, Help, WeSave and ICH.
   - RJ Stewart is Richard Stewart Jr.'s son, per an SEC filing.
   - WeSave went through 19 months of SEC review with an audit, a FINRA broker-dealer and securities counsel.
   - The SEC enforcement lookup (SALI), FINRA BrokerCheck and CourtListener federal dockets show no enforcement action or lawsuit against any principal or entity.
5. **The SEC comment letters show sloppy filings, not fraud.** SEC staff found WeSave's first securities structure (ERUs and RSUs) to be security-based swaps and told it to withdraw. Its first filing attached an auditor consent from a different auditor for a different company (OriginClear). WeSave restructured the offering and was later qualified.
6. **The technology sits in a separate, one-year-old LLC, not in ICH.** Intelavation LLC is an Oregon LLC formed 2025-05-22. Its office is a Phoenix apartment and its registered agent is **John D Wilson** of Eugene, OR. It has 5 members, whose names are behind a login. Nowhere is there public evidence that "John Wilson" has FPGA, hardware or AI experience, and "CADY", "The Thinking Chip" and "A Sovereign Thought" have no public trace at all.
7. **The documents conflict on who owns the IP.** The Thinker says "all IP… owned by ICH". The Milestone sheet instead brands the work "INTELAVATION LLC AND WESAVE". No IP assignment from Intelavation to ICH is on record. The Milestone also lists Hanson as CEO, but he has been President and CEO of Maxwell State Bank since July 2025.
8. **Net:** this is not evidence of fraud. It does back the LP's "nothing is proprietary to them" concern: the sponsoring group has no history in hardware or AI. ICH also did this before: from 2019 it marketed a defense contractor's radio technology as its own "Spectrum" future (§e). Whether CADY is worth anything depends on Wilson, what Intelavation owns, and the IP paper (§g).

## (g) Five questions to put to the company, ranked

1. **(P1) Who is John Wilson?** We need his full legal name, CV, and prior FPGA/ASIC work we can check (employers, patents, papers, shipped designs). Is he the John D Wilson who is registered agent of Intelavation LLC? Who are Intelavation's 5 members or managers and what does each own? *This settles whether any proprietary technology exists and who holds it.*
2. **(P1) Show the IP chain.** We need the executed assignment or licence of CADY IP from Intelavation and/or Wilson to ICH. The Thinker says ICH owns everything; the Milestone brands the work "Intelavation LLC and WeSave". Also: which third-party IP cores or open-source code are inside CADY, and on whose FPGA hardware? *If there is no assignment, ICH has nothing to license.*
3. **(P1) Who is raising money, and on what terms?** The Milestone mentions a "funded roadmap", yet no Form D exists for ICH or Intelavation. Which entity is raising, under which exemption, at what valuation? Separately: why have WeSave's Reg A 1-K and 1-SA reports and its Reg CF C-U not been filed, and are ICH and WeSave back in good standing in Nevada? *This decides whether we can transact with these entities at all.*
4. **(P2) Do earlier investors have claims on CADY?** Source's roughly 82 investors from 2014 and its 2015–19 investors, plus WeSave's Reg D, Reg CF and Series P holders, may have funded CADY or own part of it through the ICH stake. *Possible encumbrance and conflict.*
