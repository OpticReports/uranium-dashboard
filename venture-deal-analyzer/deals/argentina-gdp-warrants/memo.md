## The trade, honestly stated

You are buying a thrice-litigated contract claim at ~31% of its simple-
interest face value (~$16/warrant), where: (a) an MTD ruling expected
by ~summer 2026 has not appeared; (b) survival then requires winning
the merits (methodology + limitations defenses) by ~2027; (c) winning
then requires collecting from a sovereign whose identical EUR judgment
has produced no voluntary payment in 22 months; UNLESS (d) Argentina
short-circuits everything with a tender/settlement to clean its curve
for NY-law re-entry — the one genuinely attractive path, supported by
the macro leg (normalization is real: EMBI ~425, B3/B-, market access
ladder). Milei's government just won the YPF reversal and settles only
selectively (Attestor terms undisclosed). Timing of (d) is unknowable
and the fund's 12–24mo life vs merits-2027+ mismatch means fresh money
is underwriting an extension it hasn't been offered.
