Archimedes: what a 24 GHz seeker on an 80 mm aperture can physically do

Radar-range-equation output for the Archimedes 1A as described (24 GHz, 80 mm module, FPV interceptor nose). Every curve is computed, not quoted; assumptions are listed under each figure.

Sources for the anchors used above.
  • Shahed-136 RCS 0.01–0.1 m², aspect-dependent — drone-warfare.com counter-UAS analysis; CSIS Missile Defense Project notes honeycomb RCS-reduction structure.
  • 24 GHz, 38 dBm, 25 dBi, 20 × 20 cm horn → 1 km on a DJI quadcopter — Sensors (PMC6308692), “Long-Range Drone Detection of 24 G FMCW Radar”.
  • COTS transmit power: TI AWR1642 12.5 dBm, AWR2944 13.5 dBm (TI datasheets).
  • Beamwidth anchors: 100 mm aperture → 2° at 94 GHz, 7° at 35 GHz (RF Essentials, PGM seeker RF design).
  • Echodyne EchoGuard, a K-band ESA drone radar with a far larger aperture and ~33 W, is specified at ~1 km on small drones.
