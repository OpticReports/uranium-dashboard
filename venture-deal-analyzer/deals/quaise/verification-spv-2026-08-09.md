## 6. "Bounded bear" ($250mm floor, ~50% downside cap) — REFUTED

- 10–15x EV/EBITDA is rich for IPP/power gen; contracted generation
  typically 8–12x, merchant lower.
- The method counts revenue and ignores cost: 50 MW of FOAK superhot
  geothermal plausibly needs **$250–500mm+ of capex** (Quaise is seeking
  another ~$100M in grants/debt for Obsidian on top of the Series B).
- Early-stage development pipeline is normally marked at **$100–300/kW
  ⇒ ~$5–15mm for 50 MW**, not $250mm.
- PPAs carry milestone/termination rights: **if MMWD fails the contract
  does not become an asset — it evaporates or becomes a liability.**
- Conclusion: downside is NOT bounded near 50%. Equity in a pre-revenue
  FOAK driller can go to zero.
