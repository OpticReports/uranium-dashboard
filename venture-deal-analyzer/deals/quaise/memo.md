## Page 1 — synthesis

**Verdict: PASS AS PITCHED — two documents can flip it.** This is a real,
seriously-backed frontier company (Prelude re-led; JERA, Idemitsu,
Mitsubishi, Nabors on the cap table; $134M Series B first close Jul 7,
2026). But the SPV pitch fails the analyzer on three independent grounds:
(1) the technology gap is the widest the rubric has scored — deepest
demonstrated hole 118 m vs 10–20 km required, with 400–500°C completions
that do not commercially exist, and the flagship project's first phase
uses conventional drilling that does not validate the core tech;
(2) the entry is un-underwritable — no disclosed valuation anywhere, a 4x
spread between secondary marks ($5.31–$22.32/sh), unknown fees, unknown
share class, wrapped in deadline/scarcity mechanics; (3) the headline
claim as pitched ("$600M Google offtake + $24B framework MOU") exists
nowhere in the public record — the verified reality is an unnamed
hyperscaler PPA for 50 MW of the conventional-drilling phase.

**The two flip documents:**
1. **The executed Google agreement.** The LP has spoken with the parties
   and affirms it is real — that retires fabrication, and it is noted,
   not discounted. What the document must now show: counterparty page,
   **binding-offtake vs framework language**, dollar commitments,
   conditions precedent, COD dates, damages. Sector base rate: every
   hyperscaler "up to $X" framework with pre-commercial tech —
   including Google–Fervo, non-binding per Fervo's own SEC filings —
   has so far converted to zero delivered MWh. A named, binding $600M
   Google offtake would be sector-unique and re-scores traction
   immediately.
2. **The SPV's entry price and share class vs the July 7 institutional
   close.** The steelman is precise: this SPV is rational only at or
   below the institutional Series B price, in the same or senior class,
   with total fees ≤2/20 — buying alongside JERA's diligence. At
   secondary-mark-style pricing (~4x spread), it is not, regardless of
   what the Google document says.

### Why the technology score is a 2 (the depth gap)

Demonstrated (verified): 100 m (Jul 2025) → 118 m (Sep 2025 public
demo, missed its ~130 m target), 4-inch bore, vertical, dry granite.
[CLAIMED] "approaching 1 km" (Jul 2026). Required: 10–20 km,
directional, wet/varied formations — a 100–200x scale-up — plus
casing, completion, logging, and cement rated for 400–500°C that do
not commercially exist. Named skeptics on record: Roland Horne
(Stanford), Jefferson Tester (Cornell). Timeline record: 2022 promised
500°C energy wells by 2026 and coal repowering from 2028; reality is
zero energy produced and first power targeted 2030 — via conventional
drilling.

### Competitive position (the uncomfortable table)

Fervo: IPO'd May 2026 with contracted revenue, ~100 MW online Oct 2026,
~500 MW by 2028, published 70% drilling-time learning curve — on boring
oilfield rigs. Sage and XGS each carry 150 MW Meta PPAs. Mazama drills
toward superhot at the SAME volcano (Newberry) with conventional rigs.
Quaise's differentiation exists only at depths its technology has not
approached; at accessible superhot depths it has no wedge. The
hyperscaler premium window (2027–2030 delivery) goes to whoever ships
inside it — which is not Quaise's full-tech timeline.

### The "IPO in 2–4 years" claim

Treated as a risk, not a feature. Fervo IPO'd WITH revenue and operating
assets. A pre-revenue Quaise listing is a story-stock — the reference
class (SPAC-era pre-revenue energy) is dominated by >80–90% drawdowns
and bankruptcies. "Less dilution because pre-IPO" assumes the IPO;
the base rate does not.

### Pre-mortem (2034, <1x — ranked)

1. **Full-depth tech never closes the gap** (plasma breakdown, ablated
   rock removal, nonexistent 500°C completions) — Quaise becomes a
   conventional-drilling also-ran competing with Mazama at the same
   volcano on a burn-heavy cost structure.
2. **FOAK overrun kills Obsidian economics** — NuScale/Vogtle pattern;
   PPA conditions precedent lapse; down rounds crush the SPV's class.
3. **Structure/entry destroys an okay outcome** — SPV entered at
   secondary-type marks in a junior class; company muddles to an exit
   that returns institutions 1–2x and the SPV <0.5x.
