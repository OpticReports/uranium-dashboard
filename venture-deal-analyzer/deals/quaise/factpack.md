## TECHNOLOGY STATUS (verified vs promised)
- [VERIFIED] Millimeter-wave/gyrotron drilling from Paul Woskov's MIT PSFC
  research. Demonstrated to date: **deepest-ever mm-wave hole = 100 m
  (Jul 2025), extended to 118 m at a Sept 2025 public demo** (target was
  ~130 m), 4-inch diameter, dry granite quarry, vertical only; 30–40 ft
  in basalt on a Nabors rig. 100 kW gyrotrons; 1 MW system tests planned
  2026. [CLAIMED] "approaching 1 km" (Jul 2026).
- Commercial requirement: 10–20 km depth (~100–200x demonstrated), larger
  bore, wet/varied formations, directional capability (none today).
- [VERIFIED] Unsolved downstream engineering: plasma breakdown in
  waveguide; ablated-rock removal at km scale; casing/completion/logging/
  cement rated for 400–500°C supercritical conditions **do not
  commercially exist**; the touted vitrified-glass self-casing is
  unproven at depth/pressure.
- [VERIFIED] Named independent skeptics: Roland Horne (Stanford): "burning
  holes in rocks is impressive. However, that's not the whole of what's
  involved in drilling." Jefferson Tester (Cornell): "very aspirational";
  well completion at depth "may be something very, very far off."
- [VERIFIED] Timeline slippage: 2022 promises (500°C energy wells by 2026,
  coal-plant repowering from 2028) → reality: zero energy produced, first
  power now targeted 2030 at Project Obsidian — **whose initial 50 MW
  wells use CONVENTIONAL drilling to accessible superhot zones; the
  flagship phase does not validate the flagship technology.** Nevada
  coal-retrofit remains an exploratory study.
