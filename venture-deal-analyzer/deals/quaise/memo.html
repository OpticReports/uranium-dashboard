The load-bearing fact: the depth gap

Every published demonstration is ≤118 m, 4-inch bore, vertical, dry granite. The commercial thesis requires 10–20 km, directional, through wet and varied formations — with casing, completions, logging tools, and cement rated for 400–500°C that do not commercially exist. The flagship Project Obsidian's first 50 MW uses conventional drilling: the money being raised does not validate the technology being sold.
