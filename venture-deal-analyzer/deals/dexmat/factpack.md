- Sponsor blurb: "50x stronger than copper" → [PARTIAL] best published lab

  basis; commercial Galvorn SKUs spec below lab records. Basis unstated.
- Sponsor blurb: "1,000x more flexible" → [CONFLICT] company's own marketing
  says 100x flex life vs copper.
- Sponsor blurb: "approximately equal conductivity" → [CONFLICT — BASIS]
  true only per unit MASS (~75–80% of copper's specific conductivity;
  Galvorn spec 5,000 S·m²/kg vs Cu ~6,650). Per unit VOLUME: commercial
  Galvorn ~11% of copper; best-ever lab result 10.9 MS/m ≈ 19% of copper
  (Cu = 58–59 MS/m). Absolute copper parity is undemonstrated anywhere.
- Sponsor blurb: "improve conductivity by 20x" → [HEROIC] 20x over current
  commercial (~6 MS/m) implies ≥2x beyond copper absolute; even 20x on any
  basis exceeds every published result. Rice's observed trend: performance
  doubles ~every 3 years (⇒ 20x ≈ 13 years at trend). No identified
  theoretical limit, but no demonstrated path either.
- [VERIFIED] Field still advancing (2025 doped-DWCNT records, non-DexMat);
  DexMat does not make its own CNT feedstock — upstream supplier dependence.

## Market & competition (independent)
- [VERIFIED] Copper wire/cable ~$160–215B/yr; ~21.5 Mt of copper into wire.
  Beachhead: aerospace wire & cable $1.9–2.9B, ~6% CAGR; silver-plated
  mil-spec wire retails at effective $100s–$1,000+/kg for fine gauges —
  beachhead already pays 10–100x copper metal price. Value of saved weight:
  ~$2,500/kg rule-of-thumb (up to $13–20k/kg lifetime fuel) aviation;
  $1k–10k/kg launch, ~$100–200k/kg satellite build cost. EMI shielding
  market $7.4–9B. Wiring is typically a top-4 heaviest aircraft system.
- [VERIFIED] Why-now: LME copper record $13,387/t (Jan 2026); IEA projects
  ~30% copper supply gap by 2035; CNT powder industrialized (LG Chem 6,100
  t/yr; OCSiAl 100 t/yr SWCNT); methane-pyrolysis carbon-negative narrative
  (Shell-adjacent). Counter-current: EV/aero lightweighting dollars today
  flow to aluminum/CCA (40–50% harness weight cut at commodity prices);
  JPMorgan sees Al at up to 6% of copper demand by 2030.
- [VERIFIED] Direct competitors nearly absent: Nanocomp/Huntsman (only
  serious CNT-yarn predecessor) stalled — acquired 2018, 30t pilot
  announced 2023, publicly quiet since, no commercial CNT wire after 6+
  years. OCSiAl/LG Chem et al. sell powder, not conductors (potential
  suppliers/entrants). Veelo (CNT EMI veils) closest US comp in the
  aerospace-EMI wedge. Ultra-conductive copper efforts (EU UltraWire,
  ORNL Cu-CNT) never commercialized. Incumbent (Prysmian/Nexans/Southwire)
  lightweighting response = aluminum products, not nanocarbon.
- [VERIFIED] Aerospace qualification: 5–15 years, $2–10M+; wire needs
  per-spec/per-gauge QPL under AS22759 and NO slash sheet exists for CNT
  conductors (spec creation, not just entry). EMI braid/space demos carry
  lighter qual burden → only near-term revenue.
- [VERIFIED] Base rates for advanced-materials startups: Sila 10 yrs/$900M+
  to first (niche) revenue; Boston Metal ~12 yrs to first industrial run;
  Zoltek 26 years to exit ($584M). Modal failure: scale-up capex wall,
