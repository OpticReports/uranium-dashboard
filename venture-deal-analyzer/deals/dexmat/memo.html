The load-bearing fact: which conductivity basis?

The entire "replace copper" framing depends on the basis of comparison. Per unit mass (what matters when weight is the cost), Galvorn is nearly there. Per unit volume (what matters for general wiring), it is not close, and never has been, anywhere.

Pre-mortem — "it's 2033 and the SPV returned <1x"

  1. The conductivity ceiling held. Galvorn stayed a specialty EMI/space material (a tens-of-$M market) while the entry price capitalized the copper-killer story; 3–4 scale-up rounds of dilution did the rest.
  2. The qualification wall. Primes waited for an AS22759 slash sheet that waited for a committed prime; revenue stayed at R&D-sample scale; bridges priced flat-to-down past 2030.
  3. Aluminum ate the use case. Harness weight was cut 40–50% by Al/CCA at commodity prices through incumbents' qualified channels — record copper prices accelerated aluminum, not CNT.
Red team's best pass case: an 11-year-old company disguised as a 2019 seed-stage startup, whose core sales metric holds only per unit mass; the wire thesis rests on a 20x claim that exceeds every published result and arrives ~2039 at the field's own trend; the one predecessor went quiet after 6+ years; time-value alone kills the SPV math.

Conditional flip — the checkable path to yes

Invest becomes defensible only if the sponsor materials show both:

  1. A priced round at sub-$30M valuation — buying the mass-basis specialty business that already works, with the copper moonshot free rather than being what you paid for; and
  2. At least one named, signed aerospace/defense development agreement with committed volumes on the EMI-braid wedge — the only revenue path inside a fund life.

Open items — required from sponsor

  1. The four DocSend documents exported as PDF/XLSX (deck, DD-call notes, comp set, revenue/dilution model) — DocSend bot-blocked this run.
  2. Round size, valuation/cap, instrument, ownership offered, use of funds, runway.
  3. Absolute numbers behind the percentages: current $/kg, kg/yr capacity, the 2025 revenue base under "2.5x growth."
  4. IP position: Rice license scope, exclusivity, composition-of-matter vs process coverage.
  5. Any signed development agreement, LOI, or offtake — named, not "advancing toward."
  6. Reconcile: founded "2019" vs verifiable 2015–16 operations; "1,000x more flexible" vs the company's own 100x; the conductivity basis used in the deck.
