## EXECUTIVE READ (8 lines)
1. **No prospect is confirmed by either side** (memo: "Nothing is signed yet"). Of the six names, one is related-party (Moxy, via bridge investor Greg Racz), one is mislabelled (Ranpak is a paper-packaging supplier, not a 3PL — REFUTED), and one is an Apptronik investor/pilot partner (Mercedes).
2. **The company's own roadmap says customer torsos ship (Jan 2027) 23 months before Clone's first VLA (Q4 2028).** Pilots in 2027–28 will therefore be teleoperated or scripted. That is normal in the sector (1X "Expert Mode", Apptronik teaches by teleop), but it voids the memo's payback table, which assumes 85% of labour is saved.
3. **The roadmap has already slipped.** Q3 2026 "Bimanual Torso 4" is "about to be finished" on Oct 27, and our record has it launching in November. That makes roughly 5 of 5 dated public commitments missed or late: hand kits 2022, legs 2024, Alpha pre-orders 2025, the "$50M round" of Apr 2026, and Torso 4 in Q3 2026.
4. **Durability:** the Jan 2027 customer torso is rated at 100K cycles, 1/100 of the Q4 2028 production target. At bench duty that is about 13–26 working days of muscle life. Neither the price nor the payback models that maintenance.
5. **Money exists in the sector, but for evidence.** H2 2026 Series A rounds are large: Humanoid $152M at $1.35B (Jul), Eka $150M at $1.45B (Oct). Both leads priced strategic customers or manufacturing partners, and Clone has none signed. A $70–100M A at ≥$200M pre-money means 26–33% dilution and requires Torso 4 to land in the November demos.
6. **Timing (memo's own words):** the bridge closes Nov 3, Series A demos start the week of Nov 9, and Golding sees the robot in person on Nov 12. His only pre-close check is "Torso 4 run live over a Zoom". **LPs wire before the sponsor or any Series A investor has seen the robot in person.**
7. **Policy: the memo's "may require… American components" is the wrong tense.** The FCC listing has been in force since 2026-07-28. It defines "foreign-produced" via the Buy American test: made in the US with >65% domestic component cost, rising to 75% from 2029. Our record's fix ("US production or a Conditional Approval") was therefore incomplete. 7 robot Conditional Approvals exist as of 2026-10-23, none of them a humanoid.
8. **Net:** traction (demand) holds at 1.5 with downside, delivery holds at 1.5, and deal & price drifts down (wire before visit; a REFUTED sponsor claim). G1 is more at risk. No forecast moves materially. The waiting option (re-enter at the A) gets stronger, because the A demos happen 6–9 days after our money is committed.

## CONFLICTS WITH OUR RECORD

**In scope:**
1. **FCC fix incomplete.** Record §6: Polish-built robots "need US production or a Conditional Approval"; "2 robot approvals as of 2026-09-18". Correct version: US manufacture **plus** >65% domestic component cost (75% from 2029), or a DoW Conditional Approval. There were 7 robot approvals at 2026-10-23, none a humanoid (DA 26-1154).
