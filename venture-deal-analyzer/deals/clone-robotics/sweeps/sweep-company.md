# Clone Robotics: public-record sweep (company scope)
Swept 2026-10-27. Tags: VERIFIED (primary source) / SECONDARY / CLAIMED (company or sponsor only) / CONFLICT / NOT FOUND (where searched) / REFUTED / ANALYST.
A "VERIFIED (filing)" tag means the company said it in a securities or registry filing. The fact that the filing exists is verified. What it says is the company's own statement, made under liability.
Memo numbers marked (?) in BRIEF.md were not used as facts.

## 1. Executive read (8 lines)
1. **Entities (VERIFIED).** Clone Inc. is a Delaware corporation, incorporated 12 Oct 2021, SEC CIK 1958550. It owns 100% of CLONE sp. z o.o., KRS 0000947589, Wrocław. The Polish company was formed by notarial deed on 28 Dec 2021 and registered on 24 Jan 2022. The Mountain View hub rests only on CEO remarks (SECONDARY).
2. **The departed muscle co-founder is almost certainly Łukasz Koźlik (ANALYST, on VERIFIED evidence).** Clone's own Form C credits him as the person who "designed and developed custom, miniaturized, hydraulic valves and durable McKibben muscles". In Feb 2023 he was the largest shareholder (3.0M shares, 43.54%). The official Polish registry removed him as President of the Polish operating subsidiary in an entry dated **18 Mar 2026** and installed Radhakrishnan in his place. Aggregators still list him as CTO, which looks stale.
3. **IP: the "patented" claim is unverified and inconsistent with the record.** Form C (Feb 2023) says "does not have any registered patents"; all IP is trade secrets held via assignment and confidentiality agreements; a patent application was planned for 2023. I found no published patent or application under Clone, Koźlik or Radhakrishnan in the indexes I could reach (NOT FOUND). Koźlik's muscle and valve work from 2014–2021 predates the company.
4. **Financing: the only primary filing is the withdrawn 2023 Reg CF.** At that point Clone had $525k of SAFEs at a $50M post-money cap, and on 31 Jan 2023 it had $74,275 cash, about 3 months of runway. No Form D exists for any Clone entity. Every later round, and the ~$17M total, comes from aggregators or CEO remarks (SECONDARY). The "$50M round underway" of Mar/Apr 2026 has no reported close, and the company is now raising a bridge (ANALYST).
5. **Product.** Hands since 2022, then torsos, then Protoclone (Feb 2025: hung from a frame, cannot walk). No legs or biped hardware is public. Torso 4 is promised for Nov 2026 (company X post). Clone Alpha (279 units) has no price, no checkout and no deliveries. The pre-order page still reads "Pre-orders available in 2025" as of 27 Oct 2026 (VERIFIED).
6. **Casey's sensing question, public record only.** Published specs list:
   - one hydraulic pressure sensor per muscle or valve (35–36 in the 2022–23 hand; 320 in the Alpha spec);
   - 16 magnetic joint-angle encoders in the hand;
   - 70 IMUs and 4 depth cameras (Alpha spec).

   Together that is actuator-level force sensing, which the company calls "Myofiber proprioception." **No fingertip tactile or contact sensing appears in any Clone spec I found.** Casey is right about touch sensing. He is wrong if he means Clone has no grip-force feedback at all (ANALYST).
7. **Pilots.** Mercedes, VW, AST SpaceMobile, P&G and the hotel group: none is publicly confirmed by either side (NOT FOUND). Mercedes-Benz has VERIFIED its own investment in Apptronik and is running pilots with Apptronik's Apollo, which makes "Mercedes" the least plausible of the claimed logos. The memo itself says "no signed customers."
8. **Licence and acquisition: NOT FOUND in any public source**, including "Adlated". It can only be verified from deal documents. Track record: missed public dates on hand kits (late 2022), legs (2024) and Alpha (2025), plus a co-founder exit in 2022 and another in 2026.
