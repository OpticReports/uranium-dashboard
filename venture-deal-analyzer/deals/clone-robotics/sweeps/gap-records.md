## 1. Executive read (8 lines)
1. **Clone has never been shown to close a priced round.** Every known instrument is a SAFE ($50M post cap 2021–22; a $30M crowd cap in 2023, withdrawn). Every aggregator labels later rounds "Seed", "Seed VC-III" or "VC Round". None says Series A, none shows a valuation, and there is no Form D. **Last public mark: the $50M post-money SAFE cap (2021–22).** No institutional mark exists after 2022 (NOT FOUND / ANALYST).
2. **"$7.14M (Caplight)" vs "$17M (CEO)" is probably a timing gap, not a contradiction (ANALYST).** $0.64M of angel money (Freethink, Feb 2023) plus a $6.5M seed (Dealroom) comes to exactly $7.14M. Caplight lists its 2026-03-10 round with no amount, so it cannot be in that total. The ~$9.9M difference fits the unsized 2025 round (CB Insights 2025-01-01; PitchBook 2025-02-26) and the March 2026 round. **No grant and no external revenue was found that would explain it.**
3. **Seed investors, re-checked.** Initialized: VERIFIED on its own portfolio page. Tango: SECONDARY, Fund I plus a Fund II follow-on. Pioneer and Access: SECONDARY (Caplight). Wikus: SECONDARY (CB Insights' 2025 round). Lux: weakest, aggregators only. **No primary source says Initialized led, and none gives an amount or cap.**
4. **"$50M round underway" (8 Apr 2026): no close found.** The Rundown calls it only "a reported $50M raise". It is absent from the visible H1-2026 robotics and Poland funding lists (both paywalled, so this is weak evidence). Clone's EDGAR record is unchanged since 2023-04-26, and the bridge has no press. The base case is that it did not close and the bridge replaces it (ANALYST).
5. **Grants: none found** for CLONE sp. z o.o. in English or Polish web searches covering NCBR, PARP, FENG, SMART and EIC. The EU and Polish grant registries could not be queried (403/JS), so that part is **NOT SEARCHED**. The Polish subsidiary's revenue grew +48.7% (2023) and +249.8% (2024) with 79% ROE (EMIS). That looks like intercompany cost-plus billing to the parent, not grants or customer revenue (ANALYST).
6. **Pilots: none confirmed by either side.** Searches covered AST SpaceMobile, VW, P&G, Mercedes, hotels and Clone's own X account. AST is hiring human soldering technicians for its Midland expansion, and no robot is mentioned. P&G ran a 2025 open call for embodied AI on arms and cobots, which is one plausible route to a "P&G pilot" that is a challenge entry rather than a contract (ANALYST).
7. **Patents: still zero, across a wider search.** Google Patents (which also indexes PL publications) returned nothing relevant for exact-name inventor searches on Koźlik (both spellings), Michał Baran, Mateusz Wieczorek, Amadeusz Świerk, Juliusz Tarnowski and Dhanush Radhakrishnan, for assignee Clone, or for PL artificial-muscle filings. The 2023 filing plan and the "patented" claim therefore remain unsupported. Espacenet and WIPO returned 403 and UPRP was not queried directly, so those stay **NOT SEARCHED**.
8. **Licence and acquisition: no public trace.** "Adlated" and "Automaton Robotics" (Koźlik's pre-2021 project name) turn up no transaction. The memo fragment may mean a licence taken *in* or granted *out*, and the counterparty and its relation to Koźlik can only come from documents. **2026 press:** Torso 4 is promised for November after a "6-month" ground-up redesign, with no price, ship date or customer. No 2026 report of departures or layoffs was found. Koźlik's board exit (KRS, 2026-03-18) is still the only trace.
