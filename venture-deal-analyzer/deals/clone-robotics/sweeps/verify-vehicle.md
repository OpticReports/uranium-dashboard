## 2. Strongest point the sweep MISSED in the deal's favour

**The sponsor is a real, capitalised investor with his own money in robotics. He is not an empty shell.** (ANALYST, on VERIFIED filings)
- Golding bought **10,811 shares in Forward Industries' $18.50 PIPE (≈ $200k)**, at a San Juan PR 00907 address ([424B5](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/38264/000168316825007923/forward_424b5-resale.htm)).
- He holds **10,000 RoboStrategy (BOT) shares** ([N-2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2081119/000121390026093143/ea0302803-01_n2.htm)).
- Three further softeners the sweep framed as negatives:
  - The fee quote moved **5% → 2%, in LPs' favour**.
  - Two of three late Form Ds coincide with **brand-new CIKs**.
  - The vehicle Clone Red Beard Ventures Ltd is very likely an Echo SPV into Clone. That would mean Clone **did raise money in March 2026** on the same platform that runs Figure, 1X, Apptronik and NEURA SPVs.
- None of this proves close certainty or custody quality. It lowers P(sponsor is unserious).

## 3. Strongest point the sweep MISSED against

**Quaise's own filings show no new investor during Delta4's raise window.**
- Quaise's sold amount rose **$45,473,356** between the July Form D and the September D/A ($133,791,666 → $179,265,022).
- The investor count stayed at **49** ([Jul D](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1813993/000181399326000003/primary_doc.xml), [Sep D/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1813993/000181399326000004/primary_doc.xml)).
- Delta4's Quaise SPV targeted a 2026-08-12 close inside that window. A direct Delta4 investment should have added a holder, and none appeared.
- That is better evidence than the sweep's "could not fit" logic for the pattern **"Delta4 pitches; no close is visible"**. For a $50k wire into a series LLC with no adviser filing, close certainty is the gating risk.
- **Secondary, against:** RoboStrategy, which Golding holds, has ~**28%** of NAV in Figure and Apptronik (13.9% + 7.5% + 6.9%, as of 2026-07-31) ([N-2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2081119/000121390026093143/ea0302803-01_n2.htm)).
- Golding's Form D address, "1250 Ave Ponce de León, 3rd floor (Piloto)", is the same floor as Tyrell Ventures Funds (Andrew Kang; Apptronik series SPVs I–VIII) at "Suite 301" ([Tyrell Form D](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2141929/000214192926000001/primary_doc.xml)).
- So the "robotics OEM" on his site may be a competitor. That is a small conflict to disclose, not a finding (ANALYST).
