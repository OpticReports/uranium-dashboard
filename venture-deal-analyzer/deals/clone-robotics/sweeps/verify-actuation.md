# Counter-agent review: sweep-actuation.md (Clone Robotics)

Reviewed 2026-10-27 by an independent counter-agent; I did not write the sweep. Every primary source below was re-fetched by me today.
**Overall verdict: APPROVED WITH CORRECTIONS.** The arithmetic reproduces exactly. Four corrections change the read:
- the "water hydraulics = roadmap" headline overreaches;
- the pump-spec attribution is wrong;
- the best durability benchmark is at less than half Clone's pressure;
- Clone's 320/70 sensor spec is not what the demonstrated robots carried.

## 1. What changes the read

1. **The demo fluid is now settled from Clone's own words. Hydraulics is not "roadmap only".**
   - Clone's YouTube description for Torso 2 (2024-12-06): "These Torsos use pneumatic actuation with off-the-shelf valves that are noisy from the air exhaust. Our biped brings back our hydraulic design with custom liquid valves for a silent android." — VERIFIED — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gl0GnzPIOl4 (description read via https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=UCd0xLOw6No5IAsq3Y2-b0eA)
   - So Torso (Oct 2024) and Torso 2 were **pneumatic: VERIFIED**. Interesting Engineering's Nov 2024 "water-powered Torso" is **REFUTED** by Clone's own description.
   - The *hand* was water-hydraulic in 2022–23, and the sweep missed this:
     - New Atlas (2022-10-06): "a 500-watt, 145-psi water pump through a series of 36 electro-hydraulic valves". https://newatlas.com/robotics/clone-robotics-realistic-hand/
     - 80.lv (2022-09-15): "operated by water and consumes 200W at peak". https://80.lv/articles/anthropomorphic-robotic-hands-with-artificial-hydraulic-muscles
     - Clone YouTube (2023-02-17): "actuated with Clone's proprietary, hydraulic muscles and valves". https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_QUiB4MTKWk
   - Correct framing: **water hydraulics was shown at hand/forearm scale in 2022–23. It has not been shown publicly at whole-body scale.** Protoclone V1 (Feb 2025) is still a CONFLICT. Futura and humanoid.guide say pneumatic; Live Science says water, but it copied the website's product text. Clone's own Protoclone description names no fluid.
2. **The pump spec is Clone's own wording, not the press's.** The site says "a 500 watt electric pump … able to pump liquid at a 40 SLPM volumetric flow rate and 100 psi **rating**". Source: https://clonerobotics.com/android/ , JS bundle `/_nuxt/D8GOK2Ld.js`, sha256 c9b3f543…c715c, re-hashed today.
   - The sweep's line "the article's own wording, not attributed to Clone" is **REFUTED**.
   - "Rating" means a maximum, so the spec never claimed a single operating point.
3. **The best durability benchmark does not transfer to Clone's pressure.** The Kobayashi et al. 2024 full text is retrievable from the same t2r2 URL; the sweep thought it had been shut down.
   - s-muscle's 1M-cycle figure is "1 million times at **0.3 MPa** when arranged linearly", on air. 0.3 MPa is 43.5 psi.
   - Bent around a 10 mm rod, "All normal artificial muscles endured 1,000,000 cycles at 0.3MPa". But "When 0.7 MPa was applied, all the normal artificial muscles failed", and "the higher the pressure, the more likely the artificial muscles were to be damaged".
   - Clone's reported 100–145 psi is 0.69–1.0 MPa, which is 2.3–3.3x the benchmark pressure.
   - Source: https://t2r2.star.titech.ac.jp/rrws/file/CTT100925838/ATD100000413/ (IEEE RA-L 9(11):9685–9692, DOI 10.1109/LRA.2024.3455890)
4. **The 320 pressure + 70 inertial sensor spec is the product page, not the demonstrated hardware.**
   - Clone's own captions: Torso 2 had "182 sensors for feedback control" for 910 fibres (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gl0GnzPIOl4). Protoclone had "over 200 sensors" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H7dhwFcuUn0).
   - The site says Clone runs "with only proprioceptive and visual feedback" (https://clonerobotics.com/android/). That is a first-party statement that it has no touch sensing, not just an absence of evidence.

## 4. Missed points
**Strongest missed in Clone's favour.** Water-hydraulic Myofiber hands already worked in 2022–23:
- a 500 W, 145 psi water pump with 36 electro-hydraulic valves, about 200 W at peak (New Atlas; 80.lv);
- "hydraulic muscles and valves" (Clone, Feb 2023);
- the 2024 pneumatic torsos were, in Clone's words, a stopgap: "Our biped brings back our hydraulic design with custom liquid valves", and Torso 3 reportedly has in-house valves.

So the move to water is a return to a design Clone has run at hand scale, which is where its edge is claimed. It is not an untested roadmap item. Kobayashi also shows that bent-state failure is mainly a *pressure* problem with known engineering fixes: at 0.3 MPa all bent muscles reached 1M cycles, and adhesive partitions give up to 14,000x.

**Strongest missed against.** The durability evidence the sweep leaned on in Clone's favour was obtained at **0.3 MPa air**. At **0.7 MPa**, roughly Clone's 100 psi rating, *every* standard thin McKibben muscle bent around a joint-like radius failed before 1M cycles, and failure rose with pressure. Clone's own figures put it at 0.69–1.0 MPa. Nothing public shows Myofiber surviving at that pressure.

Runner-up: the sensor suite Clone advertises (320 + 70) was not on the demonstrated units (182; 200+). Clone's own copy confirms the robot has "only proprioceptive and visual feedback", with no touch.
