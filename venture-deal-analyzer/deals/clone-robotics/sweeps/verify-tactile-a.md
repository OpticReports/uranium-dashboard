## 2. What the sweep missed

**Strongest missed IN FAVOUR: pressure is Clone's *control* variable, not just a force estimate.**
- A pressure-regulated McKibben muscle is a compliant actuator whose force is **commanded and capped** by its pressure setpoint, to within model error (row 9). "How hard am I gripping" is therefore set directly, without touch. A position-controlled geared motor cannot do that: 1X says "friction swallows the contact forces" at 100:1–200:1 gearing.
- Sensorless soft hands grasp on this principle: RBO Hand 2 implements the Feix grasp taxonomy (https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1177/0278364915592961), and so does the Pisa/IIT SoftHand (https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1177/0278364913518998).
- The leaders' own architecture backs it. Boston Dynamics relies "on proprioception for agile dexterous behaviors" and adds touch for "small contact signals" (therobotreport 2026-10-01). 1X's backdrivable joints give "feather touch to a firm grip" before skin is added.
- So touch is a complement layered on transparent-actuator proprioception, and Clone has that base layer ("back-drivable, antagonistic muscles", hand page).
- This weakens the LP's premise that a hand *must* have touch to know grip force. It does not touch the slip, contact-location and fragile-object gap.

**Strongest missed AGAINST: "every muscle has a pressure sensor" rests on a spec sheet; shipped hardware has far fewer sensors.**
- Torso 2 (2024-12-06): **182 sensors** for 910 muscle fibres and 164 DoF.
- Protoclone V1 (2025-02-19): "over 200 sensors" for "over 1,000 Myofibers" and "over 200 degrees of freedom" (both Clone YouTube RSS).
- Even the Alpha spec has 320 pressure sensors against about 1,000 fibres, so at most 1 sensor per about 3 fibres.
- The Torso hands got joint-angle feedback only around April 2026 (LinkedIn).
- A Clone engineer described teleoperation "without needing feedback" (2024-12-20, https://hackernoon.com/training-musculoskeletal-androids-with-neural-control-systems-by-maciej-bakowicz).
- So the sweep's "false" half ("each artificial muscle has a pressure sensor", exec item 2, verdict section) is **demonstrated only for the 2022 standalone hand**. Even there the press is in conflict (35 vs 36 sensors). It is not shown for any Torso hand.
- The honest verdict is closer to: no touch disclosed; per-muscle force sensing is **claimed in the Alpha spec, unproven in shipped hands**.
