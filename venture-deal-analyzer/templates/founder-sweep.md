# Founder background sweep — standing protocol (v1, 2026-08-09)

Run on every scored deal's founders + key executives + any DEPARTED
co-founder, at fact-pack stage, BEFORE the panel scores team. The
sponsor-claim verification finds lies; this sweep finds omissions.
Results go in the fact pack with standard provenance tags. "Searched,
nothing found" sections are mandatory — a clean sweep is evidence and
must list the exact queries/databases that came back clean.

## Checklist (per person)

1. Identity & role from public record (company site, press, award
   records) — never from the deck alone.
2. Career history: claimed vs verifiable (press, archived pages, award
   PIs, patent filings as employment evidence). Explicitly test any
   quantified bio claim ("$XM exits", "built Y to $Z").
3. Prior ventures & endings: state registries (TX SOS, DE name search),
   shutdown/acqui-hire press.
4. Litigation: "[name] lawsuit / v.", CourtListener, Justia, state
   courts; the company itself as plaintiff/defendant.
5. Bankruptcies / liens / UCC where free-searchable.
6. Regulatory: SEC EDGAR (filings, bars), FINRA BrokerCheck where
   relevant, export-control actions for dual-use tech.
7. IP hygiene: USPTO/Google Patents — assignment chain (university →
   company?), exclusive license evidence, inventors no longer at the
   company and what they retain.
8. Grants: sbir.gov / NSF / DOE / service labs — totals, terminations.
9. Publications (technical founders): are they authors on the papers
   the technology claims descend from?
10. Departed co-founders: when (patent/press/LinkedIn-public dating),
    dispute signals, retained equity/board per any filing.
11. Adverse media: "[name] fraud / dispute / fired / resigned".

## Output format

Per person: findings tagged CONFIRMED / UNVERIFIABLE / ADVERSE with
links → "SEARCHED, NOTHING FOUND" list → "Material to the team score?"
section mapping findings to the rubric (pedigree-capped, candor-priced).

## Limits (stated, not hidden)

No LinkedIn-authenticated data, no PitchBook, no paid background
databases, no reference calls (human channel — the LP's). PACER behind
paywall: use free mirrors; note gaps.
