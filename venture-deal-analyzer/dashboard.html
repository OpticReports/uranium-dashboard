The instrument

Fixed weights, anchored 1–5 scales, scores committed independently before any aggregation. Hover each segment for why it's weighted that way.

Market 30Market & why-now — 30. Outcome data says weight the horse: tracking firms from business plan → IPO, business lines almost never change while most founder-CEOs are replaced (Kaplan-Sensoy-Strömberg, J. Finance 2009). "Why now" must be falsifiable — timing explained ~42% of outcome variance in Bill Gross's 200-company analysis, and industry-vintage effects are huge (52% of 1983 computer startups IPO'd vs 18% of the 1985 cohort). Team 30Team — 30. VCs rank team #1 (47% call it the single most important factor — Gompers et al. 2020, 885 VCs), but this rubric scores execution evidence, not charisma: performance loads on general ability + execution skills, not interpersonal traits (Kaplan-Klebanov-Sorensen, J. Finance 2012). Structured, anchored scoring predicts at r≈.42 vs r≈.19 for the unstructured meetings VCs actually run (Sackett et al. 2022). Moat 15Product & moat trajectory — 15. At early stage only counter-positioning and cornered resources are assessable; scale/network/switching powers get built later (Helmer's 7 Powers stage mapping). So the rubric scores the moat sequence, not today's moat — and asks whether the incumbent's rational response is self-harming. Trac 10Traction — 10. Provenance-weighted: verified > claimed, and sector-overlay gates apply (paid offtakes for deep tech, flattening cohorts for consumer, burn multiple/NDR for SaaS). Sponsor-sourced numbers are scored skeptically until documents verify them. Comp 10Competition — 10. Three rings: direct, adjacent, incumbent response. ~74% of incumbents respond only after year two — the question is whether the wedge compounds inside that window. Crowded ≠ disqualified: market pioneers fail ~47% of the time and hold ~10% long-run share; Google was ~the 12th search engine. 5Deal & price — 5 at seed. Seed entry price is a second-order return driver (AngelList: across a 5x valuation spread in seed deals, no difference in markup rates or multiples) — so it's mainly an ownership/fee-drag input. But the rubric switches on full entry-multiple discipline at Series B+, which is why Quaise's unpriced entry scored a unanimous 1.
fact pack + provenance tagsEvery number is tagged VERIFIED / CLAIMED / SPONSOR / CONFLICT / UNKNOWN. Defense against the two documented LLM failure modes: hallucinated quant (~8–15% on unsourced financial claims) and garbage-in from founder-provided data. 5-scorer independent panelSame fact pack + rubric, five independent runs, median + IQR reported. This is a reliability gate (IQR > 1 on any dimension = rubric ambiguity, fix and re-run) — never a training signal. A system can be perfectly repeatable and consistently wrong ("reliability without validity"). red teamA standing adversary prompted to kill the deal — a genuinely adversarial dissenter, not an assigned role (assigned devil's advocates measurably underperform authentic dissent). Counters LLM sycophancy toward the pitch narrative, observed in ~58% of cases across frontier models. Red-team and panel disagreements are logged, not averaged away. pre-mortem + WWHTBT"It returned <1x — why?" Prospective hindsight improves risk identification ~30% (Mitchell-Russo-Pennington 1989). The bull case is decomposed into what-would-have-to-be-true conditions graded evidenced / plausible / heroic — conjunctive heroics are where deals die. forecast ledgerProbabilities anchored to base rates (~65% of venture deals return <1x; ~4% return ≥10x — Correlation Ventures, 21,640 financings) and logged before the decision. Resolved on schedule; Brier-scored per rubric version. This is how the methodology becomes provably good rather than plausibly good.

Calibration & validation

Live forecasts: none resolved yet — first resolutions August 2028. Retrospective pilot (2026-08-09, n=12 YC W21/S21, blinded & QA-audited): pairwise c-stat 0.585, 95% CI [−0.44, 0.67] — indistinguishable from chance at this n, as pre-registeredA shakedown run, not a verdict: 8/12 blind scorers flagged possible recognition of the company (training-data contamination), the label spread failed stratification (only 1 of 12 raised beyond seed), and scores compressed around 3 — three protocol findings now locked as fixes for the n=60 run (obscurer sampling frame stratified on funding outcomes, harder neutering, forced pairwise scoring).
