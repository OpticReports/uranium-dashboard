# Probability-weighted net return trees (EV) — v1.1, 2026-08-09

One explicit expected-value tree per ledger deal, net of vehicle fees,
built ONLY from logged numbers (panel + red-team forecasts, memo branch
analysis). v1 was counter-agent audited; the corrections below are
applied (audit verdict at bottom). Display source for the dashboard's
"Probability-weighted returns" panels.

## Method

- Branches partition outcomes into the ledger's bands: <1x, 1–3x, 3–10x,
  ≥10x (gross). P(<1x) and P(≥10x) are the logged forecasts; middle
  splits: panel per Correlation Ventures band shapes; red team per the
  memos' LOGGED splits (audit finding 3/4 — no ad-hoc splits).
- Net-of-fees (2/20 SPV): net(g) = 0.8g + 0.2 for g>1 (exact for 20%
  carry; the ~2% one-time fee is the gap to the memo's "≈12.5x gross for
  10x net"); net(g) ≈ 0.9g for g≤1 (conservative drag, no carry).
- EV = Σ p × net. Panel and red team shown separately — the spread is
  information. Tail-multiple sensitivity shown for BOTH deals.

## Honesty box

- Subjective probability trees: the p's are logged forecasts, not
  frequencies. EV is decision support, not a return forecast; ledger
  resolutions (2028/2033+) are the only accuracy test and these EVs
  will be scored against them.
- Middle-band splits for the panel are assumption (Correlation-shaped),
  not evidence. Red-team splits are logged, not assumed.
- Not modeled: time-to-liquidity discounting (2.8x over ~12 years ≈ 9%
  IRR), follow-on reserves, partial secondaries, portfolio correlation.
- Recovery multiples (0.15x/0.10x) are charitable averages for
  deep-tech failure; setting both to zero lowers DexMat panel EV to
  ~1.56 and Quaise panel to ~1.25 — conclusions unchanged.
