Honesty box

What this is. A descriptive state variable, in the sense the Chicago Fed uses for the NFCI. Not a forecast, structurally — the module carries is_forecast=False and a gate test fails if that ever changes.

Basis. Expanding-window percentile ranks and year-on-year changes. No statistic uses data dated after the reading it produces; the load-bearing gate test appends arbitrary future data and requires every past reading to be byte-identical. Zero fitted parameters — every constant is published precedent (Hamilton 2018, OECD CLI, Conference Board) or a prior declared before results were seen.

Sample. ~5 independent US merger waves since 1980; the scored window here holds about two and a half. Effective degrees of freedom ≈ the number of cycles, not the 139 quarters. No claim about hit rates on past turning points is statistically meaningful at this N, and none is made. Gärtner & Halbheer (2009) find the canonical 1980s wave is not even significant under regime-switching — if wave existence is method-fragile, wave phase is more so.

A defect found and fixed. The momentum axis was originally Hamilton's 5-year difference. That is a cyclical-component horizon, not a rate of change, and it made both clock axes measure the same thing — corr(level, momentum) = +0.822, collapsing the clock onto its diagonal with "early" occupied once in 123 quarters. Momentum is now a year-on-year change; the correlation is +0.478 and the quadrants populate. A regression gate pins the two horizons apart.

What was NOT modeled. Transaction multiples — EV/EBITDA paid is not obtainable free with real history, so no multiples axis exists. Deal value in dollars, for the same reason. Deal premia. Structural change in the M&A market (sponsors, private credit, antitrust regime) — the percentile transform mitigates but does not remove non-stationarity. Component dependence: Harford (2005) shows aggregate capital liquidity drives clustering, so credit spread, lending standards and financial conditions are not independent readings, and the breadth measure correspondingly overstates agreement.

Where it will fail. The same place the LEI failed: a narrow, concentrated move in a subset of components reading as a broad turn. Breadth is published to catch it. Read the phase as a state, not a prophecy.
