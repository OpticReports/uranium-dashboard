# Retrospective cohort pilot — results (2026-08-09)

Protocol: 12 YC W21/S21 companies (stratified by current status, famous
outliers excluded) → deal-time packet reconstructed from pre-2022 sources
→ QA counter-agent strips provenance, fixes leakage/neutering, judges
re-identifiability → blind scorer applies rubric v1.1 (seed vector) →
independent outcome labeling (L0 dead · L1 alive-no-A · L2 raised A ·
L3 B+/acquired/≥$250M). Run as a 49-agent pipeline; round 1 was
invalidated by the QA layer itself (sources embedded in packets defeated
blinding — protocol fixed, re-run from cache).

## Headline numbers

- **All scored (n=12): Spearman ρ = 0.18, pairwise c-stat = 0.585,
  95% bootstrap CI [−0.44, 0.67].** Statistically indistinguishable from
  chance, exactly as pre-registered for a shakedown at this n.
- **Clean subset (scorer did not recognize the company): n=4** — too
  small for any statistic (c-stat 0.50).
- Gates: never fired (no G1/G3 candidates in-sample).
- Weighted scores compressed into 2.60–3.70 — poor discrimination.
- Only one company in the sample reached L2+ (the L3 was scored 3.10,
  mid-pack — one data point, not evidence either way).

## The three real findings (all protocol, all fixable)

1. **Recognition contamination is pervasive: 8 of 12 blind scorers
   flagged that they suspected the actual company** even after QA
   neutering. YC companies are heavily represented in training data.
   Fixes for the n=60 run: (a) draw the frame from obscurer populations
   (Form D first-time filers, non-YC accelerators) where recognition
   rates should collapse; (b) keep the recognition flag mandatory and
   analyze clean/contaminated separately; (c) harder neutering standards
   (the QA layer's round-2 edit logs are a ready-made checklist).
2. **Label distribution failed stratification: 5×L0, 6×L1, 1×L3, zero
   L2.** Sampling on YC "Active/Inactive" status does not stratify on
   funding outcomes — Active mostly means alive-without-Series-A. The
   n=60 frame must stratify on funding outcome directly (pull round data
   first, then sample).
3. **Score compression** — nearly everything lands at ~3. The anchors do
   not force discrimination on ordinary companies (both scored deals in
   the live ledger also cluster at 3s). Candidate fixes for v1.2:
   forced-choice pairwise scoring (Michigan-style) instead of absolute
   scores, or decile-calibrated anchors ("top 10% of seed companies you
   have seen" = 5).

## What this pilot does and does not establish

Does: the pipeline works end-to-end (blinding, QA enforcement, labeling,
stats); the QA counter-agent layer catches real protocol violations
(round 1); the cost model holds (~50 agents, ~1 hour, ~$0 data cost).
Does not: validate or invalidate the rubric — n=12 with a broken label
spread and 2/3 contamination cannot move any belief about weights. The
c-stat CI spans chance and useful-signal alike.

## Next run (n=60) — changes locked before launch

1. Frame: Form D first-time filers 2021 H1 + YC W21/S21 minus famous,
   stratified 20×L0 / 20×L1 / 20×L2+ using funding data gathered before
   sampling.
2. Neutering: apply round-2 QA edit checklist at reconstruction time;
   QA layer retained as enforcement.
3. Scoring: absolute rubric scores AND forced pairwise comparisons
   within randomized triplets (pairwise survives compression).
4. Analysis pre-registered: primary = pairwise c-stat clean subset;
   secondary = Spearman all; contamination reported as a headline
   number, not a footnote.
