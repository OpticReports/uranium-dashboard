## Anchor amendment applied (rubric v1.1, 2026-08-09)

Team anchor gains: "Pedigree earns capped credit: prior exit outcomes
count; degrees and brand-name employers alone do not (Rebel disclosure:
master's/PhD uncorrelated with success; Davenport: backgrounds are
systematically over-weighted)."

## Corrections log (counter-agent enforced)

1. McCarthy 82.5% = founder-vs-employee discrimination, NOT success
   prediction; "5x industry / 2x company age" ratios absent from paper.
   DESIGN.md corrected accordingly.
2. Scott-Shu-Lubynsky effect: +6pp per SD (≈+21% relative), R&D-intensive
   sectors only; "weak and insignificant" (not zero) elsewhere;
   generalist-equals-specialist claim unverifiable.
3. StepStone vintage span: two decades, not four.
4. GKLS rates: 30% / 21% / 22% (the 18.7% appears in one sub-spec only).
5. FFTrees "match", not "beat", regression.
6. Kerr-Lerner-Schoar: improvements in survival/employment/exits but NO
   effect on follow-on financing — relevant to our L2 label choice: angel
   selection signal shows up in operations, not necessarily next-round.
7. Dataset side (from the parallel verification): Crunchbase terms bar
   model training (Casey has ruled our use is internal study, not
   training; noted); GALI license is academic/non-proprietary — their
   application process adjudicates; Form D negatives are strategically
   biased, not noise; power replan — moderate effects (ΔAUC≈0.05)
   detectable at 400–800 resolved deals, small effects (0.03) need
   1,000+.
