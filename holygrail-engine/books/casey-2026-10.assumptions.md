  - Loans use coupon less expected default loss.
  - The ETH carry uses trailing-year funding.
- **Composer series are IN-SAMPLE BACKTESTS** of today's logic. Their backtest CAGRs (24-231%/yr, table below) are shown only to label them. They feed no number in the book.
