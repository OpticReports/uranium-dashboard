CAVEATS
  - trade-close basis: P&L is booked at exit_ts and spread pro rata over held bars; MTM would differ.
  - in-sample: same fixture the strategies were selected on; not a forecast.
  - S4 fee is 12 bps round trip in this harness (core.py sets fee_bps=2*taker explicitly); known conservative bias.
  - 'share of P&L' is a share of a compounded log return; when the blend total is small the ratio is unstable.
  - a NEGATIVE share means opposite bars carried net losses in-sample. It is an ATTRIBUTION of the
    realized path, NOT a counterfactual: forbidding coexistence would change entries, exits and the
    equity each leg sizes from. Pricing a specific coexistence rule needs its own replay.
  - a bar is 'opposite' only while BOTH legs hold; the executor's exposure to netting also includes
    the one_leg->opposite transition bars (entries/exits), which this bar count does not single out.
