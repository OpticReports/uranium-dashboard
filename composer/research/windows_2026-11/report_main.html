Windows and moving averages in Holy Grail, the sleeve and the book

Study of 2026-11-20 (addendum 59). {{n_all}} variants declared before any was run: every window in Holy Grail and the crash sleeve, simple and exponential averages, then the three-engine book. Long history from the start. In sample; nothing here is a forecast.

  • No window or moving-average change is supported in either symphony. 0 of {{hg_n}} Holy Grail variants and 0 of {{sl_n}} sleeve variants pass the five tests set in advance.
  • Holy Grail's windows sit on flat ground. Crossover pairs near 10/20, the 20-day cash test at 10 to 50 days, the SPY gate at 100 to 300 days and the pick ranking at 8 to 14 days are all within noise of the live rule. Moving the RSI tests off 10 days leans worse; allowing for all the tries it is distinguishably worse only for {{hg_worse}}.
  • Exponential averages are no better than simple ones. Same windows, EMA minus SMA: {{ema_txt}}. Where both trend tests are slowed two to five times the EMA loses less than the SMA ({{ema_hc}}), but every such variant is still behind the live rule.
  • The sleeve's windows are level too. Its IEF-against-PSQ test is level from 8 to 12 days. Stretching the ladder's one-day shock tests to 2, 3 or 5 days is behind on every stretch (t {{sg_t}} since 2020-12, {{sg_tm}} on the 26 rebuilt years before), though not distinguishably on the short verdict sample.
  • At book level nothing moves. The best single swap in each engine changes the book by {{book_rng}} log-points a year (0.10 is roughly 10% more growth a year). {{book_ci}}
  • By-product: every engine looks rougher on rebuilt history than on its real years (all simulated). Holy Grail fell {{hg_r_dd}} in 2011-12 and {{hg_m_dd}} in 2001 (real years: {{hg_a_dd}}). The sleeve made {{sl_m_cagr}} a year with a {{sl_m_dd}} fall on 1994-2020 (real: {{sl_a_cagr}}, {{sl_a_dd}}). The book's deepest falls: {{bk_falls}} (real years: {{bk_a_dd}}).
  • By-product: the sleeve reads as uncorrelated with the other two, not as a hedge of them. 1. What was varied, and on what history

{{t_design}}

Rebuilt years use the repo's long panel: a fund before its launch is its index times its leverage, bond funds are older mutual funds, VIX funds come from the VIX futures curve. "Shape only" years are those where a leg is a model of a market that did not trade (VIX futures before 2004) or, for the sleeve, where the KMLM signal it reads is the rebuilt fund that missed its fidelity bar (addendum 58): by the rule set in advance that moves the sleeve's 1994-2020 to shape only. The verdict is read on the two later stretches together. "Matched" means a threshold was re-set to fire as often as the live one, using only early years (1991-2006 for Holy Grail, 1994-2008 for the sleeve).

3. The crash sleeve

Live, in two halves. Half one: hold the inverse funds (LABD, TMV) when the 8-day RSI of IEF is below the 45-day RSI of PSQ, each only while its own 20-day RSI is below KMLM's 10-day. Half two: a ladder of one-day bond and credit shocks with RSI tests at 10 to 16 days. It has no moving average, so only windows were varied.

Is it a hedge?

Not of the other two together. It is close to uncorrelated with them: it earns about the same on their worst days as on any other day, and more on their best days. Against Holy Grail alone it has paid on the bad days since 2021, much less so on the long history.

6. What this does not settle

  • Small gains are invisible. The typical variant needed {{hg_mde}} log-points a year in Holy Grail and about {{sl_mde}} in the sleeve to stand out half the time. "No evidence for a change", not "proof the live windows are best".
  • Measurement basis: daily close to close, trades filled at the same close, 5.9 basis points per unit of turnover (about 15% above what Composer's own backtest implies; the same for every variant). Each engine is corrected for its own number of tries, not for all {{n_all}}.
  • The real years are in sample for the live rules. Their authors chose the windows on roughly those years.
  • Rebuilt funds are index times leverage: financing, fees and tracking are approximations. On Holy Grail's rebuilt 2006-2015 about a fifth of sessions hold a fund before its launch. Before 1998 several leveraged funds are the same rebuilt series. The sleeve's verdict sample is short ({{sl_ver_yrs}} years) because its KMLM signal before 2020-12 is the rebuilt fund.
  • The sleeve's ladder thresholds were tuned by its authors; matched thresholds for rare events (a 6% day in TLT) are noisy.
  • Not modelled: intraday paths, taxes, capacity, near-ties between ranked picks; decisions are simulated at the close.
  • The hedge reading is on 69 bad days since 2021 and on rebuilt history; neither is a clean test.
  • HARV was not studied (being closed).

8. Method notes for next time

  • Run the long history first. Both engines' real years were their best years; the rebuilt years are the fairer test of a rule that was chosen on the real ones.
  • Gate the simulator on Composer for variants in each family before reading anything, including one exponential form.
  • When a window change alters how often a test fires, test it both ways (matched and raw) and set the matched level on years the verdict does not use.
  • For a hedge, score the variant on the bad days of what it hedges, not only on its own growth.
