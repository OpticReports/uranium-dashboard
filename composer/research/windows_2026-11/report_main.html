Windows and moving averages in Holy Grail, the sleeve and the book

Study of 2026-11-20 (addendum 59). {{n_all}} variants declared before any was run: every window in Holy Grail and the crash sleeve, simple and exponential averages, then the three-engine book. Long history from the start. In sample; nothing here is a forecast.

  • No window or moving-average change is supported in either symphony. 0 of {{hg_n}} Holy Grail variants and 0 of {{sl_n}} sleeve variants pass the five tests set in advance.
  • Holy Grail's windows sit on flat ground. Crossover pairs near 10/20, the 20-day cash test at 10 to 50 days, the SPY gate at 100 to 300 days and the pick ranking at 8 to 14 days are all within noise of the live rule. Moving every RSI test off 10 days is worse, clearly so at 5 and at 17 to 20.
  • Exponential averages are no better than simple ones. Same windows, EMA minus SMA: {{ema_txt}}.
  • The sleeve's windows are level too, and its one-day shock tests should stay one-day. The bond switch is level from 8 to 12 days. Stretching the ladder's one-day returns to 2, 3 or 5 days is worse on every stretch (t {{sg_t}} on the verdict years, {{sg_tm}} on the 26 rebuilt years before them).
  • At book level nothing moves. The best single swap in each engine changes the book by {{book_rng}} log-points a year. {{book_ci}}
  • By-product worth knowing: all three engines look rougher on rebuilt history than on their real years. Holy Grail fell {{hg_r_dd}} in 2011-12 and {{hg_m_dd}} in 2001 (real years: {{hg_a_dd}}). The sleeve made {{sl_m_cagr}} a year with a {{sl_m_dd}} fall on its rebuilt 1994-2020 years (real: {{sl_a_cagr}}, {{sl_a_dd}}). 6. What this does not settle

  • Small gains are invisible. The typical variant needed {{hg_mde}} log-points a year in Holy Grail and {{sl_mde}} in the sleeve to stand out half the time. "No evidence for a change", not "proof the live windows are best".
  • The real years are in sample for the live rules. Their authors chose the windows on roughly those years.
  • Rebuilt funds are index times leverage: financing, fees and tracking are approximations. The sleeve's verdict sample is short ({{sl_ver_yrs}} years) because its KMLM signal before 2020-12 is the rebuilt fund.
  • The sleeve's ladder thresholds were tuned by its authors; matched thresholds for rare events (a 6% day in TLT) are noisy.
  • Not modelled: intraday paths, taxes, capacity, the knife-edge of ranked picks; decisions are simulated at the close.
  • HARV was not studied (being closed).

8. Method notes for next time

  • Run the long history first. Both engines' real years were their best years; the rebuilt years are the fairer test of a rule that was chosen on the real ones.
  • Gate the simulator on Composer for variants in each family before reading anything, including one exponential form.
  • When a window change alters how often a test fires, test it both ways (matched and raw) and set the matched level on years the verdict does not use.
  • For a hedge, score the variant on the bad days of what it hedges, not only on its own growth.
