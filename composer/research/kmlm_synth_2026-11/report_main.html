8. What this does not settle

  • The rebuilt fund is the rules plus the data, not the fund. It misses one fidelity bar on the full overlap, and a fit on 2021-2024 does not prove the same fidelity in 1995. The signal rule was read off the fund's holdings; the index rulebook is not public.
  • Futures data ends 2024-03. The check against the real fund covers three years, not six. The futures files are a public research dataset whose terms were not verified.
  • Before 2008 several legs are models. VIX funds before 2004 model futures that did not trade; the euro, JGB and Bund legs are missing for part of it. Those years cannot carry a verdict.
  • Threshold variants were not re-run. The 12 pop and dip variants were calibrated on 1990-2020 data, so they have no clean long history.
  • Decisions are simulated at the close, as in addendum 57.

11. Method notes for next time

  • Rebuild the short-lived instrument first. The stand-in used before agreed with the rebuilt fund on four days in five, and gave a different picture of the live rule's past.
  • Set the fidelity bars before building, and calibrate on something other than the thing you validate against (here: the index, then the fund).
  • Check the instrument's own distribution record against the data vendor's adjusted prices.
  • Say how far back is replication and where it turns into a model; judge only on the first.
