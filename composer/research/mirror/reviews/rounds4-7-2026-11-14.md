## What the final review measured (c1aaca77 against this branch)

- Normal $50k days (no change; one-engine and three-engine rotations; with IB's lags): identical
  orders, sizes, limits, instants, books, account, alerts and notes (A: 6 shapes; B: 86 scenarios).
- 97% of the account, sells beyond the cash outside the book: when TotalCashValue updates, the same
  orders, bought sooner; when it lags through the window, c1aaca77 sends no buys (the book sits in
  cash, red alert) and this branch buys at once. The account's low never goes below its pre-run cash
  outside the documented exposures; a $5,000 withdrawal mid-run (exposure 1) ends at -$699.22, inside
  its documented bound.
- Credits and transfers: identical; with TotalCashValue frozen, c1aaca77 leaves a whole scale-up's
  credit idle, this branch invests it.
- Fri 2026-11-27 (the real brain over HTTP into the real executor): the 2024 half-day identical;
  2025-11-28's SPY volume dropout: c1aaca77 NO TRADE, this branch trades; SPY's last trade 60 or 120 s
  old with the book trading: c1aaca77 NO TRADE, this branch trades; a whole-book freeze in the last 2
  min and a whole-book volume dropout from 12:44: refused on both.
- K5 at the decision and in a /kill: see the open item below; no open-orders request is ever sent
  while one is pending, and book equals account, everywhere.
- Restart and rollback: state written by either tree boots in the other with no archive, no halt, no
  re-buy; a held run is finished across versions; the outage, parity and transfer ledgers load both
  ways. No new environment variable; render.yaml unchanged; both services redeploy on merge.

  no reconnect storm (c1aaca77: ~226 sessions an hour during a /kill flatten on a gateway that never

- K5 late versus lost (100-218/224 s late at the decision: no trade); the 326 reset churn (6 sessions
  an hour idle, 36 in a flatten); the cap's cost; no reset on a gateway FIN; a K5 page that can go out
  while no session is up (new with rounds 4-5); a lost session's info-level "traded" summary; the
  two K5 "sync" refusals share their 60-character dedupe prefix.
- Half-day: one tree ticker's print at the instant lifts SPY's recent-trade exemption (a count or
  share threshold up to 24 costs nothing on the 5,310 archive instants; not built); a whole-book
  freeze or mute in the last 2 min with SPY's trade older than 120 s and its quiet bars moved before

  the live NO TRADE reason (160 characters) loses the book clause; the refusal waits up to 10 s

  book trading) not pinned; the floor is book-level, not per engine.
- Buying guard: exposures (1) a withdrawal or fee while TotalCashValue lags, (2) a trade by hand IB
  has not reported, (3) a price correction after booking, and a bust before IB reports it - covered
  by the operating rule (no withdrawal, no trade by hand during the run) except (3) and busts;
  C-52's commission bound may not hold for a large order of a name under ~$3.40.
