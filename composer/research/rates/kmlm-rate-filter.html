Honesty box

  • Measurement basis: daily mark-to-market, no costs, no slippage. Adding the extra round-trip each veto implies would make the variants worse, not better.
  • Real-time labels: the tree decides date t on close(t−1); DGS2 through t−1 feeds that same decision. No same-day yield is used to trade its own date.
  • Baseline is the replication (CAGR +283.7%, Sharpe 2.70), not the Composer curve (+257.9%, 2.57). Daily corr 0.9959, 99.5% sign agreement; variant-minus-baseline is measured inside one simulation so the replication gap cancels.
  • Thin cells: X = +20 and +25 bps veto only 2 and 1 switches. Those rows are near-no-ops and carry no evidence either way.
  • In-sample: 827 trading days, one regime, 82 switch events. A +283% CAGR is not a forecast.
  • Not modeled: intraday fills, borrow, the leveraged-ETF path drag beyond what the price series already carry.
  • Contrary cell, reported: the inverted filter (veto on dovish moves, X = 15) beat random p95 at 97–98%. It is 8 events, is the opposite sign to the hypothesis, and is 2 hits in ~51 cells tested — exactly the ~2.5 expected by chance. Not a finding.
