1. What was tested

The sleeve weight is 50% while a state is on and 15% while it is off; the rest is split equally between Holy Grail and KMLM. The mirror swaps the two. A state read at one close moves the weights by the next close. The yardstick is a book with one constant sleeve weight equal to the switching book's own average, so a rule gets no credit for simply holding less sleeve.

Growth in log-points a year (0.10 is roughly 10% more growth a year). The sentence on the averages could be read either way, so both directions are shown for every state.

3. Leads (none actionable)

  • Less sleeve while the market is stretched up. Sample: {{su_share}} of days since 2015-06 in {{su_reg}} regimes. SPY above its 50-day average: {{su_lo}} a year over the constant book (deepest fall {{su_lo_dd}} points), p {{su_p}}, {{su_holm}} after correction; QQQ {{suq_lo}} (p {{suq_p}}). Mirror: more sleeve on those days loses about as much. Against it: {{su_lo_shape}} on 1994 to 2015, {{su_lo_wo}} without 2020 and 2022, {{su_lo_late}} one close late, and about 12 switches a year. What it would take: the same sign on the next few years of live data, since the rebuilt years do not show it.
  • Less sleeve while the market is washed out. On 1994 to 2015 the sleeve lagged the other two most on those days ({{sd_spread_shape}} a year): {{sd_lo_shape}} a year over the constant book, {{sd_reg_shape}} regimes, {{sd_lo_late_shape}} one close late. Since 2015-06 it is {{sd_lo}}. This is addendum 61's finding again (the other engines earn most after selloffs on same-close fills), and it has not shown in the years with real funds.
  • More sleeve while QQQ is above its 200-day average (less below). The same sign on both samples: {{tq_hi}} since 2015-06 (p {{tq_p}}; {{tq_reg}} regimes; deepest fall {{tq_dd}} points deeper) and {{tq_hi_shape}} on 1994 to 2015 (p {{tq_p_shape}}). Not a primary state, so uncorrected; the deeper fall alone fails the rule.
  • Less sleeve while stocks are dear (the opposite of the idea tested). Rebuilt years only: {{va_lo_shape}} a year with CAPE above its 10-year median (p {{va_p_shape}}, {{va_reg_shape}} regimes: in effect the late 1990s) and the same for stocks dear against bonds ({{v3_reg_shape}} regimes). 5. What this does not settle

  • In sample, and short for slow states. Eleven recent years hold three or fewer valuation or rate regimes; those ideas can be ruled "not shown", not ruled out.
  • Forward earnings were not tested (no point-in-time history of forward estimates is on file); CAPE and the earnings yield against real bond yields stand in.
  • One pair of weights (15% and 50%) and one threshold per state, fixed in advance and not tuned. A different threshold could look better on this sample; searching for it would prove nothing.
  • Before 2015 the funds are rebuilt and, further back, modelled (Holy Grail real from 2015-09, the sleeve from 2020-12, KMLM from 2021-06); the 2015-06 sample has no sessions from 2020-12-02 to 2021-06-01.
  • Multiplicity: {{n_states}} states, two directions, three samples; corrected only across the nine primary states.
  • Same-close fills in the engine paths; the one-close-late column is the bound. Moving money between symphonies is modelled as one session; in practice it settles the next evening. Measurement basis: daily mark-to-market; 5.9 basis points of each switch's turnover.
