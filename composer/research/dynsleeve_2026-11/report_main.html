1. What was tested

The sleeve weight is 50% while a state is on and 15% while it is off; the rest is split equally between Holy Grail and KMLM. The mirror swaps the two. A state read at one close moves the weights by the next close. The yardstick is a book with one constant sleeve weight equal to the switching book's own average, so a rule gets no credit for simply holding less sleeve.

{{t_fixed}}

Growth in log-points a year (0.10 is roughly 10% more growth a year). "SPY" is the S&P 500 index; QQQ is the fund from 1999-03 (dividend-adjusted) and the Nasdaq-100 before. "Stretched" means the distance above the average is in the top tenth of its last five years; "washed out" the bottom tenth. Each direction has its own p (a test that allows for runs of similar days: Newey-West, 63 sessions); a switch with no information loses about 0.005 a year to costs, so the two directions are not mirror images. "After 9" corrects for the nine primary states. The sentence on the averages could be read either way, so both directions are shown for every state.

5. What this does not settle

  • In sample, and short for slow states. Eleven recent years hold five or fewer regimes of the valuation and rate-level states; those ideas are "not shown", not ruled out.
  • Forward earnings were not tested (no point-in-time history of forward estimates is on file); CAPE and the earnings yield against real bond yields stand in. CAPE and CPI are today's vintages.
  • One pair of weights and one threshold per state, fixed in advance. The reviewer ran 113 other combinations of threshold, weight pair and lag on the simple-average stretch states and nine others, without looking for a winner; none passes, and none has a gaining direction with p below 0.05 since 2015-06.
  • Real, rebuilt, modelled. Real funds: Holy Grail from 2015-09, the sleeve from 2020-12, KMLM from 2021-06. Before that: rebuilt funds (Holy Grail 2006-07 to 2015-08; the KMLM fund 1991-04 to 2020-12), and shape only for Holy Grail before 2006-07 and the sleeve before 2015-06; VIX funds before their launch are a model: on the real VIX term structure from 2006-07, on a curve fitted from the VIX alone before that, and of a market that did not exist before 2004. From 2015-06 to 2020-11 the sleeve's rules run on mostly real funds. The 2015-06 sample has no sessions from 2020-12-02 to 2021-06-01.
  • Not modelled: taxes on about a dozen switches a year, each moving 35% of the book; settlement (a move between symphonies settles the next evening; one session is assumed); both books are reset to their weights daily at no cost.
  • QQQ is dividend-adjusted here; a trader sees unadjusted prices. The reviewer found that changes about 20 sessions and flips two of the smallest margins.
  • Multiplicity: {{n_states}} states, two directions, four samples; corrected only across the nine primary states. Same-close fills in the engine paths; the one-close-late columns are the bound. Measurement basis: daily mark-to-market; 5.9 basis points of each switch's turnover.
