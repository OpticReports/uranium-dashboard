How much of this year's return came from leverage

Composer account only, 2025-12-31 close to 2026-10-15 close (198 sessions; today's intraday move is excluded). "No leverage" keeps every signal, trade and day, and swaps each 2x/3x ETF for its 1x version at the holding step (TQQQ to QQQ, UDOW to DIA, SQQQ to PSQ, UVXY to VIXY, and so on). Pre-tax, which under Act 60 is also after tax.

Time-weighted, the account made +76.8%; the same trades without leverage would have made about +27.2% (range +24.2% to +27.2% across the holding and deposit-fill assumptions). Leverage added about 50 points, roughly two-thirds of the return. On your money, which mostly arrived April to July, it is +35.0% against +10.1%: $32.8k of the $46.0k gain. Sharpe came out about the same either way (+0.17, not firm).

Why the model's Sharpe falls when leverage comes off (HG, current version)

Partly placement: leverage sits on HG's best signal, so taking it off shrinks the 3x days about threefold while the hedge and bond days barely shrink and become a bigger share of the risk. Partly cost: the backtest charges the same slippage dollars to both legs, and they eat a bigger share of the smaller return. With costs the model's Sharpe goes 2.04 to 1.44; without them 2.04 to 1.44. In the live account the gap is +0.17 (90% band -0.47 to +0.93, 65% chance it is above zero).

Basis and limits

  • Actual = Composer's own time-weighted account return. It counts each deposit as arriving at the start of its day, so the 04-28 deposit's intraday loss is in the return. No leverage = that return minus each day's leverage contribution, from the holdings of the symphony version live that day, weighted on the same base. Fills, cash drag and deposit timing stay as they happened.
  • HG holds one asset at a time. On the 14 days its live return matched a different holding than the model's (a switch made a session early or late at the 15:50 rebalance, or a different branch), that holding was used. On 22 further days nothing fits cleanly; the model holding is kept and the range above spans the alternatives. The 06-30 edit of HG was never deployed: on 07-02 live HG earned exactly XLV's return, a leaf only the original version has.
  • Deposits that were invested before the close carry their own intraday leverage effect, read from the symphony's deposit-adjusted return (04-28: a $50k deposit bought TQQQ intraday and lost 7.5% by the close; two-thirds of that is leverage). The liquidated July symphony was cash on 07-01 and still held its SOXL/TECL basket through 07-07.
  • Sharpe and Sortino are annualised from daily returns over T-bills. Nine and a half months is short: the actual Sharpe's 90% band is 0.3 to 3.8. The gap between the two columns is measured on the same days and is still not firm.
  • Trading costs are held equal. 1x ETFs trade at tighter spreads, which would add perhaps a point or two to the no-leverage side. USD, LABD and SOXS have no 1x twin; their own return divided by the leverage is used. "No leverage" still holds 1x VIX-futures funds (VIXY, SVIX, ZVOL at 50 to 55% volatility), so it is not a low-risk portfolio on those days.
  • Signals were not re-run on 1x prices. The thresholds are tuned to the leveraged tickers, so that would be a different strategy. For KMLM and the sleeve "same trades" means the same weights.
  • Composer's costed backtest of the live versions at live weights reads +62.5%, below live, because of the 13 deposit and withdrawal days in the live series and the 5 bp slippage it charges on HG's 162x turnover (frictionless +94.6% against costed +79.2% for HG), not because of leverage. Composer's dashboard Sharpe of 2.11 is lifetime and includes today.
  • Composer account only; the IBKR book is not included. ZVOL is a -1x mid-term VIX fund.
