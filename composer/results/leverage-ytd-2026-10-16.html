How much of this year's return came from leverage

Composer account, 2025-12-31 close to 2026-10-15 close (198 sessions), time-weighted. "No leverage" keeps every signal, trade and day, and swaps each 2x/3x ETF for its 1x version at the holding step (TQQQ to QQQ, UDOW to DIA, SQQQ to PSQ, UVXY to VIXY, and so on).

The account made +76.8%. The same trades without leverage made about +23.7%. Leverage added roughly 53 points. It also lifted Sharpe this year, because the 3x positions sat on the strategy's best signals, but nine and a half months is too short to call that gap firm.

Actual vs no leverage, with the market for scale

UPRO is plain 3x S&P at about the same volatility as the account: leverage with no signals. Basis and limits

  • Actual = Composer's own time-weighted account return. No leverage = that return minus each day's leverage contribution, computed from the holdings of the symphony version that was live that day. Fills, cash drag and deposit timing stay as they happened.
  • HG holds one asset at a time. On the 10 days its live return matched a different holding than the model's (a switch made a session early or late at the 15:50 rebalance), the live holding was used.
  • Sharpe and Sortino are annualised from daily returns over T-bills. On nine and a half months the levels are loose: a block bootstrap puts the actual Sharpe's 90% band at 0.3 to 3.8. The gap between the columns is tighter but not firm: +0.45, with a 90% band of -0.30 to +1.34.
  • Trading costs are held equal. 1x ETFs trade at tighter spreads, which would add perhaps a point or two to the no-leverage side.
  • Signals were not re-run on 1x prices. The thresholds are tuned to the leveraged tickers, so that would be a different strategy.
  • Engine rows use the Composer model. KMLM, SLEEVE and HARV were designed in July on data that includes this year, so their first half is in sample. The account rows are entirely live.
  • Composer's backtest charges about 8 points this year on HG's 162x turnover. Live paid about 5. That gap is why the costed model reads lower than live. It does not depend on leverage.
