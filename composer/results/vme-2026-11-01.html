Value and momentum everywhere, read against this book

Asness, Moskowitz and Pedersen (2013) find value and momentum premia in eight markets, negatively correlated with each other, so that a 50/50 blend has a far higher Sharpe than either. This page tests what that means for our four symphonies: AQR's own updated factor series (1972-2025), add. 47's book history (1997-2026, rebuilt prices before launch, the rules' own years after 2015), buyable ETF proxies, and FRED's funding-liquidity spreads.

D. Funding liquidity as a gate? The book earns after liquidity worsens.

The paper's liquidity shock (AR(2) residual of the TED spread, signed as liquidity), 1997-2022. Bars: the annualized Sharpe of each series in the month AFTER a worsening shock vs after an improving one. The factors behave as the paper says (value after worsening, momentum after improving); the book and every engine do better after worsening, so a de-risking gate would remove the months they earn.
