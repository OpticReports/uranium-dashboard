Value and momentum everywhere, read against this book

Asness, Moskowitz and Pedersen (2013) find value and momentum premia in eight markets, negatively correlated with each other, so that a 50/50 blend has a far higher Sharpe than either. This page tests what that means for our four symphonies: AQR's own updated factor series (1972-2025), add. 47's book history (1997-2026, rebuilt prices before launch, the rules' own years after 2015), buyable ETF proxies, and FRED's funding-liquidity spreads.

Nine ETFs (SPY EFA EEM IEF TLT GLD DBC VNQ UUP). Each month-end: the momentum sleeve holds the top 3 by 12-1 month return; the value sleeve the top 3 by the paper's non-stock value signal (the worst 60-month return); 50/50 of the two. Growth of $1 since 2012, log scale; SPY in gray for context. The value sleeve's Sharpe moves between 0.1 and 0.3 across 48-72 month lookbacks, the 50/50 between 0.4 and 0.6 (addendum 51).

The paper's liquidity shock (AR(2) residual of the TED spread, signed as liquidity), 1997-2022; the shocks are skewed, so the legs are unequal (counts in the table).
