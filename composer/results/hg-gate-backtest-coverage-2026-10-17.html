How far the HG 200-day gate was backtested

HG with the 200-day gate on its five trend baskets, run on one daily price panel from 1990 to 2026. The leveraged funds launched between 2006 and 2015, so the earlier years use prices rebuilt from each fund's 1x ETF or index. This page shows which years are actual fund prices and what the gate did in each stretch.

What each leg's prices were made of

One row per fund HG holds or reads, with the share of sessions it held that fund. A rebuilt leg is 2x or 3x the daily return of its 1x ETF or index, minus financing at the T-bill rate, plus a cost term: fitted on the overlap years for the QQQ, SPY, DIA, XLK and semiconductor-index rebuilds; a fixed 1.8% a year for the IWM, XBI and other index rebuilds.

Basis and limits

  • Engine and numbers: the add. 42 gate lab (levgate.py). A counter-agent rebuilt the panel prices and the three variants' daily returns independently (match to 1e-15) and reproduced every era figure. Signal and trade at the same daily close; Composer trades at about 15:50. Costed at HG's measured 5.1 bp per unit of turnover, including the gate's switches.
  • Tracking where rebuild and fund overlap: every rebuilt layer of the five gated legs, QLD, TECL and SOXL is within 1.2% a year of the actual fund. Looser: USD's semiconductor x2 (+1.6% a year), LABU's XBI x3 (+1.5%) and its Nasdaq Biotech stand-in (+14%), and the VIX-fund models (UVXY +13%, VIXY +5.6%, VIXM -2.2%). Each of those funds is held 2% of sessions or less, identically with and without the gate.
  • Index rebuilds add a dividend yield (the S&P's monthly yield; 0.5% a year for the Nasdaq-100, 1.5% for the Russell 2000; none for the biotech and semiconductor indices). Where a fund's own index is not available, the Nasdaq-100 or S&P 500 stands in. VIX funds before 2011 are a regression on the VIX index, cruder before mid-2006. T-bills stand in for BIL before mid-2007.
  • The panel skips 30 bond-market holidays before 2007 (no T-bill price). Returns span those days but CAGR counts sessions, so pre-2007 CAGRs read about 0.4 points a year high, for every variant alike.
  • HG's rules were built on 2015 to 2026 data, so that stretch is in sample for them and 1990 to 2015 is not. The gate is in sample on the whole panel: add. 40 chose it (the textbook 200-day rule, the simplest of its family) after testing 15 shapes here.
  • The backtest runs HG's current version (saved 2026-07-30, live from 2026-07-31). The December 2025 version differed in its defensive branch (a 10-fund RSI filter where this one holds BIL); the trend and dip-buy logic the gate touches is the same.
