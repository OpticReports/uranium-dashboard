A long-only value / momentum stock sleeve in IBKR: what it has earned

Two kinds of evidence. Ken French's long-only portfolios (CRSP/Compustat, survivorship-free, point-in-time, value-weighted, gross of costs): big-cap rows of the 2x3 sorts, i.e. stocks above the NYSE size median in the top 30% on momentum (~300 names), book-to-market (~140) or profitability (~310), which is what an IBKR sleeve would actually hold; the concentrated 5x5 corner cells (top 20% x top 20%; the value corner is 6-31 names, bank-heavy) are shown as a sensitivity. Then the live ETFs that implement the same ideas, net of fees, as they traded. Benchmark: the US market (SPY where it exists; SPY and the CRSP market track at 0.99 correlation).

A. Growth of $1 since 1963: big-cap value, momentum and their blends vs the market (log scale, gross)

The 50/50 of the big-cap top-30% value and top-30% momentum rows, rebalanced monthly, and the thirds version with profitability. Costs are not in these lines; the table carries the net-of-assumed-cost rows, the concentrated cells and the all-cap deciles. "names" = median / latest firm count in the row since 1963.

B. The honest chart: rolling 10-year excess return vs the market

Annualized CAGR of each sleeve minus the market's over the trailing 120 months, gross (overlapping windows). Above zero = the sleeve beat the market over the previous decade. Momentum rarely dips below; value spends whole decades below; the blends sit between.

The last 21 months carry the modern-era numbers. Excess CAGR over the market (French rows) or SPY (ETFs), with the recent windows stopped at 2024-12 and then run through 2026-09, and the 2025-01..2026-09 rally months alone (annualized from 21 months). Stopped at 2024-12 the big-cap 50/50 had lost to the market since 2010 and the live MTUM+VLUE blend had lagged SPY by 2%/yr; the 2025-26 value rally put them back to even and ahead. The concentrated value cell's rally number is 9-13 names.

C. Drawdowns: the sleeve falls with the market

Drawdown from the prior peak, monthly, gross. A long-only sleeve has a beta near 1 (1.06 since 2010, 1.08 in the 2025-26 months; the concentrated value cell 1.8); it does not protect in a crash, it is a different way to hold stocks.

D. What the buyable versions actually did, net of fees
