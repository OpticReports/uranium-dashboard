Shadow runner: checking Composer's decision minute by minute

When the approved logic on closing prices disagrees with what Composer bought, the runner asks whether the logic, re-run on prices from Composer's decision window, gives what Composer holds (a knife-edge). It used to try three 5-minute snapshots from Yahoo; it now tries every minute from 15:50 to 15:55 on FMP 1-minute bars, with the old checks as fallbacks. The window is measured, not assumed: Composer's holdings match the logic best at 15:54.
