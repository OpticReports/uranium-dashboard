Basis and limits

  • allow = the share of the leveraged legs' weight that stayed leveraged, averaged over the window. TO/yr = total turnover including the leverage switches, costed at 5.1 bp per unit for HG.
  • Decisions are the engines' own; the gate only changes which share class of a leg is held. Signals are read at the prior close; Composer trades at 15:50, so live switches lag these by one session in the worst case.
  • Before 1999 the leveraged legs are index reconstructions (3x index minus financing, S&P dividend yield accrued) and the 1x twins are the same indices; VIX funds are modelled. Out of sample for HG's rules, which were set on 2015 to 2026 data.
  • About fifty factor shapes, each run on every leveraged long and on the trend baskets only, are all shown. The placebo and inverse tests are per variant; the best of a hundred is expected to beat its own placebo even when nothing works, so read the family, not the top row. On the trend baskets several families pass the long-history nulls; only the 200-day family also keeps its edge in the 2007 to 2009 window.
  • Panel A is in sample for all four engines (HG's rules were set on 2015 to 2026 data; KMLM, SLEEVE and HARV were designed in July 2026). FRED series are read on their calendar date, a one-day publication lag ignored. Composer conditions are stateless, so the hysteresis variants are shown for information only; the plain 200-day gate gives the same numbers.
  • Research pass only. Nothing here changes a live symphony.
