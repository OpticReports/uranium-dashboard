Every session the runner works out what each symphony's approved logic says to hold, on its own prices, and compares that with Composer's actual holdings. This page is its baseline: replayed against every daily snapshot of the live book since July, and against Composer's own backtests.

How each knife-edge was confirmed

Composer decides on prices from about 15:45-15:55 ET; the runner uses the official close. A difference counts as a knife-edge when the approved logic, re-run with every ticker at its actual 15:45, 15:50 or 15:55 price (Yahoo 5-minute bars), gives exactly what Composer holds. Bars go back about 60 sessions; before that, and on any day bars are missing, a one-ticker move of at most 0.3σ must explain it instead. The placebo row is the same test on deliberately wrong pairs inside the bar window.
