Futures volume and the overnight session: what the futures tape adds to the overnight signals

Add. 53 read prices only and no futures. This adds the futures tape on two horizons: the Globex overnight session itself (ES, NQ, 10-year note, gold, yen; hourly bars with volume, June 2024 to November 2026, the only free intraday futures history) and the prior session's volume (ES, NQ, the fund's own, SPY's) back to 2015 on the mornings add. 53 studied. Outcomes are the next regular session's open-to-close move of SPY and of the three tech 3x funds, and of the KMLM leg when it held one. Every split is permutation-tested; the family-wise p counts all the splits tried on a sample.

A. The 11-05 crash was not an overnight event: the futures tape was flat until 09:00

ES futures from the prior cash close, hour by hour, for 11-04 (the night before KMLM flipped bullish) and 11-05. Bars: ES volume per hour on 11-05 (the shaded band is the cash session). Overnight into 11-05 the ES was +0.04% at 09:00 on normal volume (1.0x its 20-night median) and a normal range; NQ was −0.9%, gold +1.5%. The −4.4% came between 09:00 and 16:00, three quarters of it by 11:00. No pre-open read of the futures, price or volume, had it; the funds' own 09:30 prints did (SMH gap −4.4%, SOXL −14.6%: add. 53's signal, which the engines' legs bounce from on average and did not that day).

B. The overnight tape predicts the size of the session, not its direction, and the size read is the range

582 nights. Above: the session's open-to-close move of SPY against the overnight ES move; the line is the fit over all nights (the overnight move reverses on average: slope −0.13, t −2.2; for the tech 3x funds −0.68, t −2.4), high-volume nights in orange. Below: the absolute session move by tercile of overnight volume (blue) and of overnight range (orange). Heavy overnight volume is followed by a bigger session either way, but volume and range move together, and once the range is known volume adds nothing. Neither says which way (the fit on high-volume nights is the same reversal, t −1.8).

C. Down nights: what the futures tape says about the coming session

Nights when ES was down 0.5% or more at 09:00 (n 73) and 1% or more (n 30). The session continued down about half the time, and the mean session move was positive. Each row splits the sample by one futures signal; the difference of means carries a single-test permutation p, and the family-wise p at the top of each block is for the largest difference among the splits tried, given over the splits with at least eight nights a side (the 2-6-night splits, a V already under way by 09:00, are shown as means only: the n 6 "recovered" cell at 0.5% is one night, 2025-04-09, the tariff pause, +11.2% for SPY; +1.0% without it). Nothing survives family-wise. The one split near p 0.05 single-test is the 10-year note up overnight at ES ≤ −1% (15 vs 15 nights, bonds confirming the risk-off: no bounce); volume and range splits are p 0.3-0.9.
