Overnight and pre-open overlays on the engines' leveraged legs: what an IBKR layer could have added

The engines' 15:50 decisions are left as they are. The test is what an IBKR engine could do between decisions: on the morning after a leveraged entry, read what is known before 09:30 (Asia's closes, the DAX open, VIX, the fund's own pre-open gap, the sector's gap) and exit at the open, flip into the inverse fund for the session, or sit out after a run of flips. Applied to the KMLM switcher's and HG's reconstructed daily paths (add. 47 pipeline, the live trees) 2015-06..2026-11-04 on the real funds' opens and closes, with assumed costs, placebo and inverse tests; and, model-free, to every morning after any 3x fund closed oversold since 2008.

A. Model-free: after an oversold close, the worse the overnight, the better the day

Every morning after SOXL, TECL, TQQQ, SPXL or LABU closed with Wilder RSI(10) under 30 (the engines' dip-buy trigger), 2008-2026. Bars: the fund's open-to-close move by decile of its overnight gap. The bottom decile (gaps averaging −9%) was followed by +4.7% intraday, positive 70% of the time. Pooled correlation of each pre-open signal with the subsequent open-to-close move is in the table: all negative for "bad news" signals.

B. The same rules on the KMLM switcher's own path: every exit costs return

Exit the leveraged long at the 09:30 open when the signal fires, hold cash until the next 15:50 decision; the engine's decisions are unchanged. Bars: change in CAGR over 2015-06..2026-11-04 vs the engine as built (gross of the overlay's own costs: 15 bps at the open, 10 bps to re-enter when the engine re-holds the fund). The thin bar is the placebo band (5th-95th percentile of exiting the same number of random leveraged-long mornings): a rule inside or below the band has no information or picks the wrong mornings. Labels: fires and the share of fires where exiting helped.

E. Pre-market prints vs the open (the exit price an IBKR engine could actually get)

Sixty sessions of 5-minute pre-market data (all our sources keep). The 09:25 print is the open to within 0.1%; the 08:00 print sits within 1-3% of the open and, on gap-downs of 4% or more, already shows ~70-80% of the gap. So "exit at the open" is a fair stand-in for a pre-market exit; a 04:00-08:00 exit would have sold a little higher on the big gap-downs and lower on the others.
