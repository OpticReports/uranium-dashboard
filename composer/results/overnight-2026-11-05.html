Overnight and pre-open overlays on the engines' leveraged legs: what an IBKR layer could have added

The engines' 15:50 decisions are left as they are. The test is what an IBKR engine could do between decisions: on the morning after a leveraged entry, read what is known before 09:30 (Asia's closes, the DAX open, VIX, the fund's own pre-open gap, the sector's gap) and exit at the open, flip into the inverse fund for the session, or sit out after a run of flips. Applied to the KMLM switcher's and HG's reconstructed daily paths (add. 47 pipeline, the live trees) 2015-06..2026-11-04 on the real funds' opens and closes, with assumed costs, placebo and inverse tests; and, model-free, to every morning after any 3x fund closed oversold since 2008.

A. Path-free panel: after an oversold close, the worst overnights were followed by the best sessions

Every morning after SOXL, TECL, TQQQ, SPXL or LABU closed with Wilder RSI(10) under 30 (the engines' dip-buy trigger), 2008-2026. Bars: the fund's open-to-close move by decile of its overnight gap. The bottom decile (gaps averaging −9%) was followed by +4.7% intraday, positive 70% of the time (t 4.2; +5.2% without 2020); deciles 2-9 are flat (−0.3% to +1.2%), so this is a tail effect, not a gradient. It is conditional on the oversold close: unconditionally, SOXL gap-downs of 5%+ since 2015 (n 197) show no rebound (mean −0.01%, 46% up). B. The same rules on the KMLM switcher's own path: 43 of 47 exit rules cost return

Exit the leveraged long at the 09:30 open when the signal fires, hold cash until the next 15:50 decision; the engine's decisions are unchanged. Bars: change in CAGR over 2015-06..2026-11-04 vs the engine as built (net of the overlay's own costs: 15 bps at the open, 10 bps to re-enter when the engine re-holds the fund; the signs hold before costs). The thin bar is the placebo band (5th-95th percentile of exiting the same number of random leveraged-long mornings): random exits already cost (−6.6%/yr at 100 fires, because the legs earn intraday on average); a rule below the band picks worse mornings than chance. Labels: fires and the share of fires where exiting helped. The four rules with a positive change are mirrored "good overnight" signals (table), t ≤ 1.3.

F. The other side: the morning bounce as a trade

If a bad overnight after an oversold close predicts a better session, buy it. E1: on a morning when a 3x fund closed with RSI(10) under 30 and the signal fires before 09:30, buy the fund at the open and sell at the close (one trade per day, qualifying funds equal-weighted, 30 bps round trip), 2008-2026. E. Pre-market prints vs the open (the exit price an IBKR engine could actually get)

Sixty sessions of 5-minute pre-market data (all our sources keep). The 09:25 print is the open to within 0.1%; the 08:00 print sits within 1-3% of the open and, on gap-downs of 4% or more, already shows ~70-80% of the gap. So "exit at the open" is a fair stand-in for a pre-market exit; a 04:00-08:00 exit would have sold a little higher on the big gap-downs and lower on the others.
